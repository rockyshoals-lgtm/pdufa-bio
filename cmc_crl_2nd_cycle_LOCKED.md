# CMC CRL → next-cycle outcome: LOCKED-DOWN classification (2021–2026)
Every entry verified against the **actual openFDA CRL letter text** + company primary sources.
"Second try" = approved on the very next PDUFA (one CMC CRL → approval). "Got a 2nd CRL" = drew another CRL.

## ⚠️ Why a clean "% resolve" can't be computed
openFDA single-CRL CMC apps by CRL year × status:
2021: 34 approved / 0 pending · 2022: 13/0 · 2023: 21/0 · 2024: 9 approved / 23 pending · 2025: 0/26 · 2026: 0/5.
2021–23 are ~100% "approved" by **publication bias** (FDA published older CRLs because they later approved); 2024–26 are **right-censored** (not resolved yet). So any backward % is an artifact. The reliable signal is the **count that drew a 2nd CRL**.

## HONEST VERDICT on "≈90% resolve on the 2nd PDUFA / ~10% get a 2nd CRL"
- **Overstated as a blanket number.** No clean published "% of CMC CRLs that get a 2nd CRL" exists; the openFDA corpus is biased; the only firm count is that **≥25 CMC-flagged apps drew a 2nd CRL in 2021–26** (7 genuine branded + 18 generic/biosimilar).
- **Population is everything:** large-sponsor branded novel drugs usually clear on the next cycle (though even AbbVie Vyalev took 2 cycles). Small-cap / 505(b)(2) / generic / foreign-single-CMO sterile injectables draw repeat CMC CRLs **routinely** — nowhere near a 10% rarity.
- **The real tail risk isn't a repeat CMC CRL — it's the CMC→EFFICACY flip** (Atara Ebvallo): the first "clean manufacturing" CRL bought a cycle and then exposed an unapprovable efficacy package.

## UNCY read-through
UNCY fits the favorable branded sub-population (single, clean, third-party-subcontractor CMC; no clinical/safety flags; **EU already re-inspected that vendor with no deficiencies**). That profile clears at the high end. But "no 2nd CRL on the very next cycle" is a ~2-in-3-to-favorable base rate, **not 90%** — and the residual risk is another cycle (a 2nd CRL), not a binary zero. The one thing that would make it a true counterexample (a hidden efficacy problem) is largely off the table here, since OLC's clinical/safety package was never questioned.
