## 1. Executive Summary — the harshest 10

1. **You still say "Coming soon."** The landing `<title>` literally ends "(coming soon)" and the hero reads "Coming soon · 2026," yet you have 100+ indexable pages, live data, and a working app. You are throttling your own launch and trust. Cold visitors get a teaser, not the product. **Kill "coming soon" everywhere and put a free, no-login preview of the real tape above the fold.**

2. **"LOA 87%" on the per-drug card is your single biggest self-inflicted wound.** Your entire wedge is "we never estimate per-drug approval odds" — and then every catalyst card prints a fact tile reading **`LOA 87%`** inches from the drug name. To a retail user that *is* a per-drug approval probability, tooltip or not. It contradicts the methodology page and hands competitors a "they do it too" rebuttal. Get it off the card.

3. **Your caveats are invisible on the one device that matters.** Every disambiguation — LOA-is-a-cohort-stat, "Vol rich," "Sourced," the slip note — lives in a `title=` attribute. `title=` only fires on desktop hover. On mobile (your PWA, your whole thesis) none of it is reachable. The trust layer evaporates exactly where most users are.

4. **The public per-event page is a beautifully built dead-end.** `/pdufa/UNCY` is excellent SEO — and its primary CTA is "→ pdufa.bio/app · access required (private beta)." A cold searcher reads great facts, then hits a locked door with no email capture, no "notify me," no free path. You are buying SEO traffic and venting it to nowhere.

5. **You own the literal term "PDUFA" and have no "What is a PDUFA date?" page.** The SERP for your namesake query is a junk-drawer of RTTNews, BiotechEdge, Dan Sfera, BioPharmSignal — none authoritative. A domain called **pdufa.bio** not ranking #1 for "what is a PDUFA date" is malpractice. Same for CRL, AdCom, priority review. Zero evergreen explainer pages exist.

6. **The Decisions archive is CRL-only and mislabeled.** The page is titled "Approvals & CRLs"; all 21 entries are ✗ CRLs. Zero approvals. It reads like a doom-list, misses the higher-volume "FDA approves [drug]" search intent, and undersells your verified-archive differentiator. Approvals are the *more common* outcome — show them.

7. **The public calendar is cluttered with duplicates and placeholder dates.** The same drug appears under multiple tickers (GSK *and* SPRO for tebipenem; KEYTRUDA+Padcev under PFE/MRK/ALPMY; Ziihera under JAZZ/ZYME/ONC; Camizestrant under AZN/GH). 20+ events pile onto **2026-12-31** — obvious "2H-2026 estimate" placeholders shown as hard dates. Indications truncate mid-word ("…Phosphatase and Tensin Ho", "a-home inje"). It looks unfinished.

8. **There is no retention spine.** No alerts, no "what changed since your last visit," no Silent-Shift push, no watchlist notify. HEIMDALL already flagged this as #1; it's still open. A daily-habit "tape" with nothing to bring you back is a brochure. Watchlist is a passive, date-sorted list.

9. **The gated filter bar is an 18-control wall, and the primary mode switch hides inside it.** Today/Historic — your top-level IA split — uses the same `.chip` styling as "Runway <12mo" and sits inline among ~15 other chips. The single most important navigation control looks like a throwaway filter.

10. **Contrast fails AA where it counts.** `--mut2:#7e95b6` on `--card` (~4.0:1) is used for the date line, "Sourced:" pill, fact-key labels (9px) and registry line — i.e., the provenance you most want trusted is the hardest to read. Known-open; exact fixes below.

**The good news, stated plainly:** the methodology page, the approval-rate page, and the trial-success page are genuinely best-in-class — sourced, schema'd, honest. The provenance taxonomy (source_type/confidence, validation badges, primary-source links) is a real moat no competitor has. The discipline is real. The problem is almost entirely **distribution, first-touch conversion, and a few mixed-signal UI elements** — not the substance.
