## 0. 🔴 CRITICAL — your Pass-1 fixes shipped to the BUILD but production is serving STALE HTML on `/` and `/calendar`

This is the headline. Your highest-value, most-linked URLs are serving the **old pre-Pass-1 page** to every real visitor and to Googlebot.

**Evidence (reproducible):**
- `https://pdufa.bio/` → still "Coming soon · 2026", **no** look-inside tape, old `<title>` "(coming soon)", **no** "Learn" nav.
- `https://pdufa.bio/?nocache=redteam3` → the **fixed** build: title "…PDUFA dates, run-up history & verified FDA decisions", hero "**Live · private beta**", and the **"A LOOK INSIDE — REAL UPCOMING FDA DECISIONS · FREE"** 12-row preview tape (ACHV, UNCY, ARQT… each linking to its free `/pdufa/<slug>`).
- `https://pdufa.bio/calendar` → still GSK **and** SPRO as separate rows, mid-word truncation ("…Tensin Ho", "a-home inje"), hard "2026-12-31" pile, **no** "Learn" nav.
- `https://pdufa.bio/calendar?nocache=redteam2` → the **fixed** build: "**GSK / SPRO**", "**ALPMY / MRK / PFE**", "**JAZZ / ONC / ZYME**" co-listed; "…" word-boundary truncation; "**Q4 2026 (est.)**" labels; "Learn" in nav.

**Interpretation:** The build is correct and deployed. New routes (`/learn/*`, `/decisions/approvals`) are fresh because they were never cached. The **pre-existing** routes (`/`, `/calendar`, and likely the per-event/`/decisions` pages) are pinned to a stale edge-cache entry that never got purged on deploy. A query string busts the cache key → you get the new HTML. **So #2 (kill-coming-soon + look-inside) and #7 (calendar de-dup) are NOT live for users despite being logged "SHIPPED."**

**Fix (concrete):**
1. **Purge the CDN now** for `/`, `/calendar`, `/decisions`, and all `/pdufa/*` + `/fda-decision/*` (Vercel: redeploy with cache invalidation, or `vercel --prod --force`, or hit the purge API for those paths).
2. **Add a deploy-time purge hook** so future builds don't silently strand fixes behind stale HTML.
3. **Set explicit revalidation** on HTML routes — `Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=300, stale-while-revalidate=86400` (or ISR `revalidate`) — so content can't pin indefinitely.
4. **Verify after purge:** the bare `/` shows the tape and the bare `/calendar` shows "GSK / SPRO". Until then, assume **users see your old site.**

## 2. New / sharpened issues introduced by the changes

### 2a. 🟠 The 236 new approval pages are thin — no drug, no indication, no company
*Fact:* `/fda-decision/SRPT-2025-11-25` renders: H1 "SRPT FDA Decision Approved · 2025-11-25", a T-120 chart, decision-day move (+2.3%), run-up (+90%), "Note **—**", and "price-only (validating)." **No drug name, no indication, no company name** anywhere. Compare the CRL pages (e.g. CAPR) which *do* carry drug + reason + primary source.
*Interpretation:* 236 near-identical pages of {ticker, date, "Approved", chart, move, "price-only"} is a **thin-content / doorway-page risk** (Google can discount or de-index them) and a weak user page. The H1 and `<title>` don't even name the drug, missing "[drug] FDA approval" search intent — the whole reason to publish approvals.
*Fix:* Join the drug/indication/company metadata you already have on the calendar side into each decision page. Minimum per page: **drug name + indication + company in the H1/title** ("SRPT — Elevidys… FDA Decision: Approved · 2025-11-25"), a one-line plain-English "what was approved," and **hide the empty "Note —" row** when blank. If you can't enrich all 236 before launch, `noindex` the bare ones and index only enriched ones — don't ship 236 thin pages into the sitemap (it grew 112→437 largely on these).

### 2b. 🟡 Landing overclaims the archive vs reality
*Fact:* Landing feature grid (and the `/decisions/approvals` selling point) says the archive has "approvals & CRLs **with the reason and a link to the primary FDA / SEC / company source**." But **every** approval is "price-only" with **no reason and no source link** — only CRLs have those.
*Fix:* Reconcile copy with data: "Past **CRLs** with the reason and a primary-source link; **approvals** source-linked as we verify them." Don't let the strongest trust claim be the one a skeptic can falsify in one click.

### 2c. 🟢 Look-inside tape — good, two polish nits
The tape is exactly right (T-minus, ticker, cap, drug, PDUFA date, ±cohort move, links to free pages, excludes already-passed GSK/SPRO 06-18). Polish: "**1days**" → "**1 day**" (singular) and ensure a space ("10 days"); confirm the "±3% cohort" label keeps the "cohort" qualifier on mobile so it never reads as a per-name prediction.

### 2d. 🟢 `/learn` cross-linking is thin
The flagship mentions CRLs and AdComs but doesn't link to `/learn/what-is-a-crl` or `/learn/fda-advisory-committee-adcom`; the hub doesn't link out to `/calendar`'s relevant rows. Add inline cross-links between the 4 explainers and from each to the calendar/decisions — it compounds the topical-authority value you just created.
