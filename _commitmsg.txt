seo: build the roads to the long tail, answer the questions, show the freshness

Acting on the competitive teardown. Its central finding is the one worth building against: Assyro,
BiopharmaWatch and FDA Tracker keep their catalyst data behind query parameters, so their entire
dataset ranks on one URL each. Nobody in the category has a per-drug, per-ticker or per-decision
page. We have ~377 of them. The hub-page war is crowded and we are behind on authority; the long
tail is empty and we are the only one with an army in it.

Except the army had no roads. /calendar and /decisions linked to ZERO ticker pages. That is also the
mechanical reason 421 URLs sit in "Discovered, currently not indexed": Google found them in the
sitemap, and nothing on the site argued they mattered. 405 internal links now route from every
catalyst list into the company pages, as a "Companies on this page" index. It went in as an index
rather than inline links because calendar rows are themselves <a> elements and nested anchors are
invalid; browsers silently unnest them.

FAQ + FAQPage schema on all 209 ticker hubs, which had zero. The uncontested queries are questions
("when is the SLS phase 3 readout" is Google's own People-Also-Ask), and PAA eligibility needs
question-shaped content plus schema. Every answer is generated from data we hold and show: next
catalyst at its real date precision, decision history with outcomes, measured run-up. Answer first,
evidence second, because Google and the assistants lift the first clean sentence. A ticker with no
dated catalyst gets an answer saying so rather than a plausible invention.

Freshness stamp site-wide, which attacks the single most exploitable weakness found: the category
leader ships a badge reading "Synced 1w ago". Ours cannot be seen unless we show it.

The stamp took two attempts and the first was wrong in an instructive way. Baking the build time
into 850 pages made every page's content hash change on every build, so the sitemap immediately went
back to claiming 100% of URLs changed today, the exact signal the lastmod work exists to remove. It
would also have committed 850 modified files daily, forever, for a string nobody reads. My own guard
caught it. Now the markup is constant and one small /build-info.json carries the timestamp, fetched
at view time. Page bytes verified stable across consecutive builds.

The static fallback had to be true on its own, since a crawler without JS sees only that. It reads
"rebuilt daily", a claim about the schedule that is verifiable and cannot rot. "Updated 4 hours ago"
baked into a cached page becomes a false claim about honesty, which is the worst kind to publish
here.

Sitemap boilerplate list extended to cover the stamp and the company index, so template furniture
never advances lastmod. Result on this build: 164 pages report a real content change across 5
distinct dates, which is the FAQ landing on the ticker hubs, not a sweep.
