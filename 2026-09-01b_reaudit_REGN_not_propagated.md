## Why this matters more than any other stale-page problem

**These are the highest-converting pages on the site.** From Bing:

| Page | CTR | Position |
|---|---:|---:|
| `/pdufa/NVO-mim8` | **60.00%** | 3.00 |
| `/pdufa/REGN-garetosmab` | 20.00% | 2.95 |

*(and the query `garetosmab pdufa` converts at **33.33% at position 2.00**)*

**Every one of those is stamped August 7 — 25 days stale.**

There is a second harm: `dateModified: 2026-08-07` on a page whose event has since been decided is a **freshness signal working against us**, on exactly the page type we most want engines to trust. It is also the opposite of the "4 hours ago" advantage `/calendar` holds on the SERP.

**Fix:** add per-event PDUFA pages to the daily rebuild, the same way `/drug/*` already is. The REGN content will then appear without a manual fan-out — and the same class of failure stops recurring.
