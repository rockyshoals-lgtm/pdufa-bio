## RED-TEAM OBJECTIONS

1. **CADL T-4 is unusually compressed.** A Phase 3 plenary slot at AUA was disclosed March 9, 2026; the market has had ~9 weeks to price it. Conference plenary readouts have different microstructure than PDUFAs. Don't assume the same regime-adjusted runup playbook applies.

2. **Six catalysts firing early in a 6-week window is regime-shift evidence.** Either (a) sponsors are systematically pulling forward readouts to capitalize on capital markets, (b) trial timelines are running faster than guidance, or (c) FDA's transparency-rule changes are nudging behavior. Whatever the cause, **canonical calendars dated >30 days back are now unreliable as runup-window anchors.** This is the same regime-shift signal that drove the May 1 memo on 2026 YTD PDUFA approval rates falling to 45%.

3. **AVTX, MIRM, TRDA, NTLA all reported positive topline early — selection bias risk.** Sponsors with negative data don't typically pull readouts forward. If our framework over-weights "fired early" as a positive signal, we may be picking up sponsor optimism rather than actual data quality. Reconfirm trial-design-quality scoring is upstream of any "fired-early-is-positive" heuristic.

4. **UNCY date discrepancy is a minor flag but a structural concern.** The company's own corporate communications gave two different PDUFA dates in two press releases on the same day. This suggests either internal coordination weakness or a typo never corrected. For a small-cap PDUFA play, this is a yellow card.

5. **ACHV cGMP risk is unchanged but compounding.** Adare partnership for U.S. manufacturing is post-PDUFA — i.e., it does not resolve the open observations at the current CMO. The "first new FDA-approved smoking-cessation drug in two decades" thesis is fine; the CMC delay risk is the binary one to handicap.

6. **CRDF IP litigation under-coverage.** Nerviano breach allegation disclosed February 2026. Cardiff disputes. This was not previously surfaced in our flow. If their motion succeeds, it materially impairs the onvansertib platform. Track on next scan.
