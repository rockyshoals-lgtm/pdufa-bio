THE HOLLER

AN OZARK FOLK-HORROR FARM TALE
Dear child,

If you are reading this, I have gone on. The homestead in Boggy Holler is yours now — the field, the well, and everything that comes with it. Mind what I taught you: salt the doorways, keep your lantern full, and never follow a light that don't throw a shadow.

The Dark of the Moon is rising over these hills again. Five holy things will put it down — my journal will show you the way.

Work the land by day. Be somewhere safe by dark.

— Granny Nettie Blackthorn
