# Readout mine: every guided readout date tied to the company's own words (2026-11-17)

David, 2026-11-17: "mine for accurate readout dates that are sourced" and "Go live after red team".
From the cowork readout thread to the builder. Facts only, not investment advice.

## What changed

- **277 records collected**, each from the company's own press release (newswire or IR site) or its SEC
  filing, with the exact quote, the timing words as stated, the source date and the link.
  Data file: `2026-11-17_readout_mine_sourced.json` (key `rows`).
- **Every record was re-opened by an independent red-team pass** (quote verbatim, program, timing words,
  source date, newest company release checked for a change). Verdicts: 226 CONFIRMED, 38 FIX (field values
  corrected from the page), 12 DROP, 1 HOLD.
- **Applied by `apply_1117_readout_mine.py`** (only CONFIRMED and FIX; idempotent from a clean tree):
  - 17 Guided rows marked **Reported** on the day of the company's release (MNKD, VTGN, ALNY via Vir, HELP,
    KYNB, MTVA, TRAX, ROIV, SYRE, PCVX OPUS-1, SLS REGAL, PMN, SCYX, XENE, BMEA, MIRM, RAPP).
  - 20 rows re-sourced to the newest statement, 14 rows whose window moved (date_history records each move).
  - 4 rows whose window passed with no results and no new timing: resolution `no_topline` + ack
    (AARD, PPBT, BOLT, VKTX VANQUISH-2).
  - 3 rows removed from the forward list: NMRA (the May text was a trial initiation, not a readout),
    ATAI (acquired by Lilly, delisted), SKYE (trial terminated). Not st "Withdrawn": that is the
    FDA-application status and its guard would read a readout as an application.
  - 196 new Guided rows. New rows take the company spelling the site already uses for the ticker.
- Windows are never narrowed. Half-year and looser wording publishes at year precision with the company's
  words in `_d.guided_label`; relative words ("next year") are made absolute in the label.
- 175 new rows got a therapeutic area in `_readout_ta_manual.json`, each a hand decision from the
  company's stated indication (basis `Company release: indication stated as ...`, source = the release).
  Rows with no stated indication or only healthy volunteers are left untagged.

## Display fixes

- `build_guided_readouts.py`: year/half rows printed a made-up day ("2027-12-31"). They now print the
  company's own words ("Second half of 2027"); quarter and month rows print "Q1 2027" / "Mar 2027".
- `build_ticker_hubs.py`: same fix on ticker pages for year-precision readouts.
- `build_vktx_hub.py`: the page said VANQUISH-2 data was guided to Q3 2026. Viking's Oct 28, 2026 release
  says only "The company expects these studies to be completed in 2027", so the page now says that.
