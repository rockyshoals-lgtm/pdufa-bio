# 2. 🔴 TWO READOUTS RESOLVED AND WE NEVER RECORDED THEM

I scanned all 327 readouts for events past their date with no outcome. **Four are company-`Guided`** — those are the ones that matter, because a company named the date. All four are wrong.

## TENX — Phase 3 topline landed **August 10**. We're 24 days stale.

**And we have the wrong trial name on it.**

Our record reads **"LEVEL-2 readout."** The release confirms LEVEL-2 is a *different, still-running* trial:

> *"TNX-103 (oral levosimendan) is currently being evaluated in **LEVEL-2**, a Phase 3, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial"* — enrollment completing end of 2027.

The trial that read out was **LEVEL** (NCT05983250). So either the name is wrong or the date is wrong by more than a year. Either way this row is not publishable as it stands.

**What actually happened — and this one needs care, because "missed its endpoint" alone would misinform:**

| Endpoint | Result |
|---|---|
| Primary — 6MWD at Week 12 | LS mean difference **3.5 m**, **p=0.63** — **not met** |
| Key secondary — KCCQ-TSS | difference **0.1 points** — not met |
| Prespecified subgroup, baseline 6MWD <333 m | **+26.3 m** (95% CI 6.0–46.7), nominal p=0.0112 |
| NT-proBNP, overall | **−49% vs placebo**, nominal p<0.0001 |

**Carry the company's own caveat verbatim — it is more honest than anything we would write:**

> *"Nominal p-values are not adjusted for multiplicity and these analyses do not establish efficacy."*

Tenax intends to request a **Type C meeting with FDA**. Full results were presented in a **Late-Breaking Clinical Science session at ESC Congress 2026, Munich, August 28–31** — and **we hold an ESC record dated 2026-08-28 with no TENX presentation attached to it.**

**Suggested one-liner, plain language, no verdict attached:**
> *"Tenax's Phase 3 LEVEL trial of oral levosimendan did not meet its main goal — patients walked 3.5 metres further than placebo in six minutes, a difference small enough to be chance (p=0.63). Pre-planned looks at sicker patients and at a heart-strain blood marker were positive, but the company notes these do not by themselves prove the drug works. Tenax has asked to meet with the FDA."*

## MPLT — Phase 2 topline landed **July 27**. We're 38 days stale.

MapLight's **ZEPHYR** Phase 2 of ML-007C-MA in acute schizophrenia **met its primary endpoint** — statistically significant improvement in PANSS total score versus placebo at Week 5 on the 210/3 mg BID dose. Generally well tolerated; no serious or drug-related severe adverse events reported.

Our record: `Guided`, 2026-08-31, no outcome.

## TYRA — the date is wrong by about a year

We show **SURF303, 2026-08-31**. The company guides **initial results in 2027**. SURF303 is a Phase 2a/b in low-grade upper tract urothelial carcinoma; the first patient has been dosed.

**Per the date-accuracy doctrine this is the worst of the four** — the other three are stale, this one is simply not true, and it is sitting on a public page today.

## ALZN — precision is wrong, not the fact

AL001's bipolar Phase II topline is guided to **Q3 2026** (through Sept 30), not August 31. *(The earlier AL001 "Lithium in Brain" study already read out in March 2026 — bioequivalence met — so make sure we're tracking the right one.)*

# 3. THE PATTERN — AND IT'S THE ONE WE ALREADY SOLVED ONCE

**The early-approval watcher queries FDA's feed daily for every armed PDUFA. It caught MIMRYLO 33 days early. It does not look at readouts at all.**

TENX and MPLT are **the same failure as REGN**: a dated event resolved, nobody was watching, and the page kept advertising a pending result. The only difference is that a readout has no FDA feed to query.

**But it isn't unwatchable.** Three signals are free and already in reach:

| Signal | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **SEC 8-K by ticker** | Both TENX and MPLT filed one. Every guided readout has an armed ticker; poll EDGAR for new 8-Ks on those tickers. |
| **ClinicalTrials.gov status** | We already store `nct_id` on these rows. A trial flipping to *Completed* or posting results is a lead. |
| **Guided-date expiry** | The cheapest of all: **any `Guided` readout whose date passes with no outcome is a lead by definition.** No external call needed. |

**Guard 59 — the readout analogue of `test_decided_not_upcoming.py`:** *no `Guided` readout may sit more than N days past its date without either an outcome or an acknowledgement entry.* Blocks CI, same as the PDUFA guard, and the acknowledgement file shrinks rather than grows.

*Separately: **51 `Estimated` readouts** also sit past their dates, all on mid-month placeholders (06-15, 07-15, 08-15). That's a different problem — those dates were our estimates, not company guidance, so they aren't "missed." But 51 stale rows on the readouts surface is its own trust cost, and they should either be re-estimated or aged out.*
