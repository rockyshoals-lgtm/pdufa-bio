New `inject_decision_article_schema.py` (CI after build_date_modified; chain): one NewsArticle per indexable decision page (369) with headline, description, **datePublished = the page's first commit in Eastern** (cached in `_decision_published.json`, seeded from git, committed by CI), dateModified, author/publisher pdufa.bio, `isBasedOn` = the FDA notice/letter or sponsor release, `about` = the Drug (brand + INN); plus article:published_time.
