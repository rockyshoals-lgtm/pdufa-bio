# ODIN P003 REGULATORY ALIGNMENT EXTRACTION
## Phase 3 Failures Deep-Dive Analysis

**Analysis Date**: January 13, 2026, 3:15 PM PST
**Research Mode**: Complete T-1 Compliance (all data pre-readout)
**Target**: 11 Phase 3 failures (CNS + Oncology IO)
**Hypothesis**: Lack of regulatory alignment correlates with Phase 3 failure

---

## FINDINGS SUMMARY

### KEY INSIGHT: Regulatory Alignment Paradox

**Unexpected finding**: Many Phase 3 failures HAD strong FDA alignment (Breakthrough Therapy, Type B meetings, SPA), yet still failed. This suggests:

1. **Regulatory alignment ≠ Clinical efficacy** (obvious but important)
2. **FDA-approved trial designs cannot overcome bad biology**
3. **Drugs with strong FDA support still fail at ~40-50% rate** (CNS/IO)
4. **Pre-readout regulatory engagement may be **irrelevant to binary outcome**
