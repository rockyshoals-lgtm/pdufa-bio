Run-up context, measured from a uniform T-120 baseline. Across 1,833 FDA decisions since 2020, the median peak gain from 120 trading days out was 17.8%, while the median move from T-120 to the day before the decision was 1.9%. The gap between those two numbers is the point: most of the move happens before the decision and is usually given back. 96% of events have a full T-120 window; the rest are excluded rather than back-filled.
