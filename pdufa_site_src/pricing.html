Questions

Is the calendar really free?
Yes. The PDUFA calendar, the full decision archive, and the run-up study are free with no login. The paid plans add data as files and more API room. Nothing that is free today moves behind a paywall.
Which plan fits me?
Free covers most readers. Pro is for an investor who wants each decision’s daily price path as numbers and a calendar feed for their own tickers. ULTRA is for a fund, quant or research team that wants every decision and its options history as data, with room for 2 million requests a month.
Can I buy Pro or ULTRA today?
Not yet. Neither plan takes payments or sign-ups. The features marked with a check are built and run in the API now; the rest are still being built.
