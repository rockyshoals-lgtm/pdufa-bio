Days after the period ended are calendar days from the last day of the calendar quarter, half or year to the date of the results release; they are left blank for a period the company prints as fiscal, since a fiscal period need not end with the calendar quarter. Releases read through November 27, 2026.

Every date on this page comes from the company's own press release as the newswires carried it, or, where marked 8-K, from its own SEC filing (releases read through Financial Modeling Prep's press-release feed; times are US Eastern). A report day appears only when the company's release names it, such as “will release financial results for the third quarter of 2026 after the market close on Wednesday, October 28, 2026”; a results date is the newswire time of the company's results release. No date here is estimated, and a vendor's projected earnings date is never used: where a company has not announced its next report day, this page shows none. A release is matched to a ticker only when it prints the ticker's exchange tag or its headline opens with the company's name; law-firm and shareholder-alert releases are left out. Where no newswire results release was read for a period, the company's own Form 8-K under Item 2.02 (Results of Operations and Financial Condition) can stand in, marked “8-K”: its time is when the SEC's EDGAR system accepted it, which can be minutes or hours after the release itself went out, so no day count is given for it. An 8-K is used only when its Item 2.02 text says the company put out its results for one period that had ended, not before the report day the company announced, and not when two 8-Ks claim the same period; a preliminary update or a notice of the report date is never used.
