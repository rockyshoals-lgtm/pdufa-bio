Privacy policy

DRAFT for David to review. Not live, not indexed, and not legal advice. Highlighted items in brackets must be filled in or confirmed before launch. Have a lawyer read this page before it goes live.

Short version: pdufa.bio keeps as little about you as it can. No ads, no analytics trackers, no selling or sharing of your data. If you sign up for free alerts we keep your email address and how often you want mail, and we delete it the moment you unsubscribe.

Effective date: [DATE OF LAUNCH]

pdufa.bio is operated by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy covers www.pdufa.bio, its API and its emails.

What we collect

  • Free email alerts. Your email address, the alert frequency you chose, and the times you signed up and confirmed. We do not ask for your name and we do not record your IP address with your sign-up.
  • Pro account and API keys (only if you buy Pro). Your email address, a customer ID from our payment processor and a hashed copy of your API key. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Sign-in cookie. If you sign in to a Pro account we set one cookie, pd_session, that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is not used for tracking. We set no other cookies, and none at all if you never sign in.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and troubleshooting, for a limited period.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, and our emails contain no tracking pixels and no tracked links.

How we use it

  • To send the alerts you asked for, and the one confirmation email that starts them.
  • To run a Pro subscription and API key, if you have one.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We never sell, rent or share your email address for anyone else's marketing. Alerts may mention pdufa.bio Pro; they never carry third-party advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel: hosts the site and stores the alert list.
  • Resend: delivers our emails.
  • Stripe: handles Pro payments, only if you buy Pro.

These companies act on our instructions and may store data in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • An unconfirmed sign-up is deleted automatically after 48 hours.
  • A confirmed sign-up is kept until you unsubscribe, then deleted at once.
  • Pro account records are kept while the account is active and as long afterwards as tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices and rights

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also email [CONTACT EMAIL] to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, and we will answer within 30 days. We give these rights to everyone, wherever you live, including rights under the EU and UK GDPR and California privacy law. Our legal basis for sending alerts is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.

Children

pdufa.bio is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and if the change is material we will tell alert subscribers by email before it takes effect.
