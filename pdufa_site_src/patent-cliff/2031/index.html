2031 Patent Cliff

Updated August 28, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar

102 drugs lose patent protection in 2031. The biggest single group is Cancer, with 15 drugs. Losing protection does not mean a drug disappears: it means other manufacturers can apply to sell the same medicine, and the original maker usually loses most of the sales within a year or two.

This is the earliest date a generic could enter; it is not a guarantee one will. We calculate it as the later of the last patent the company listed with the FDA and the last regulatory exclusivity. Companies often reach settlement agreements with generic makers for a different date, and those agreements are not public in this data.
