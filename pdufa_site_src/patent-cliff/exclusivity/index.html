Regulatory exclusivity cliff: 854 FDA exclusivities ending through 2031

Updated October 7, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar

854 FDA regulatory exclusivities on brand-name drugs (NDAs) end between October 7, 2026 and December 31, 2031, per the FDA Orange Book. Exclusivity is separate from patents: it runs from approval (five years for a new chemical entity, seven for an orphan drug, three for a new indication, six months added for pediatric studies) and blocks the FDA from approving certain competing applications until it ends, whatever the patents say. It is often an earlier date than the last patent. Orange Book data files dated September 11, 2026.

An exclusivity ending is not a generic launch. Patents, settlements and the time a generic needs for approval decide when a competitor actually reaches the market. This page lists the FDA's exclusivity dates only. Not investment advice.
