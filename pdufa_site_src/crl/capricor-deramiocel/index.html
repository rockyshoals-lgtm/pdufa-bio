Capricor and deramiocel: two Complete Response Letters

Page updated November 25, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 26, 2026 1:22 AM ET

Capricor Therapeutics applied for approval of deramiocel for Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) under BL 125842. The FDA issued a Complete Response Letter dated July 9, 2025, and Capricor announced a second one on November 20, 2026. This page sets the FDA's released letter beside the company's statements. Times are Eastern. Sources read 2026-11-21, 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-25. Facts only; no view on any outcome.

What the FDA's July 9, 2025 letter says

Quoted from the FDA's released letter (PDF); each quote is checked against the text of the letter in the FDA's own release (openFDA transparency/crl) every time this page is built.

TopicThe letter
Primary endpoint“The HOPE-2 study failed to demonstrate efficacy for its prespecified primary efficacy endpoint, which was change from baseline to Month 12 in the mid-level (elbow) dimension of the Performance of Upper Limb version 1.2 (PUL 1.2)”
Study design“As stated in your response to an FDA information request […] the HOPE-2 study was intended to evaluate the effectiveness of deramiocel on neuromuscular function and was not designed to assess effectiveness for the treatment of cardiomyopathy in patients with DMD” (The date of the information request is redacted in the released letter.)
Secondary and exploratory endpoints“include 50 secondary and exploratory endpoints, including post hoc analyses of 26 endpoints of cardiac MRI assessments”
Multiplicity“no prespecified multiplicity adjustment strategy was employed for control of the overall Type 1 error rate”
Conclusion“Your BLA does not meet the statutory requirement for substantial evidence of effectiveness.”
What the FDA recommended“If you wish to seek approval of deramiocel for treatment of cardiomyopathy in patients with DMD, we recommend that you conduct adequate and well-controlled study(ies), whose primary objective is evaluation of cardiac outcomes in deramiocel-treated patients with DMD cardiomyopathy, compared to a randomized, concurrent control group over an appropriate duration of follow up”
Deadline“Within one year after the date of this letter, you are required to resubmit or withdraw the application”

The letter also has sections headed “Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC)” and “CMC [Facility and Equipment]”. In the released text the pages after these headings are blank and the numbered items resume at item 13, so what the FDA wrote about manufacturing is not in the released version.
