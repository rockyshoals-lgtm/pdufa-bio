What the 2024+ letters name as deficient

157 Complete Response Letters dated 2024 or later have a PDF of their own. 155 of them have a text layer we read; 87 carry at least one deficiency section heading in the FDA's own words (labeling, safety update and other administrative sections set aside). The other 68 number their deficiencies without a section heading, so they are left out of the table rather than guessed. A letter can name several sections.

How this page is made

Inputs: the FDA's CRL release (openFDA transparency/crl, snapshot 2026-08-29), the section headings each PDF's text layer carries (_crl_headings.json), the Orange Book products file (_orange_book/products.txt), and this site's decision pages. The script is build_crl_facts.py in the site's public repository; it runs on every rebuild. A record the FDA repeated verbatim is counted once.
