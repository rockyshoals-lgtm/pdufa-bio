Questions

What is a Complete Response Letter?
A Complete Response Letter (CRL) is the FDA's formal notice that it will not approve a drug application in its current form. It lists the deficiencies the sponsor must address; it is not a permanent rejection, and many drugs are approved after resubmission.

How many FDA Complete Response Letters are published here?
444 letters, released by the FDA under its CRL transparency program and hosted on FDA's own servers. 159 were issued in 2024 or later; the rest go back as far as 2002.

Why does this page not show an approval rate after a CRL?
Because this corpus cannot answer that question honestly. Letters for pre-2024 applications were released because those drugs were LATER APPROVED, so nearly all of them precede an approval by construction. Letters from 2024 onward are too recent for many outcomes to exist yet. Counts are stated; rates from this data would mislead in both directions.
