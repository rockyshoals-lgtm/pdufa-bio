Which biotech companies are presenting at AASLD 2026?

How do biotech stocks typically trade around AASLD?
In our study of 41 AASLD presentations (2017-2026), the median stock moved -0.28% over the 10 trading days before the presentation (46% of them rose), +0.33% on the day itself, and +2.15% over the 5 trading days after. The pattern across the whole study is a drift up into these meetings and down out of them, strongest in the smallest companies. That is a historical distribution for a cohort, not a prediction for any one company, and not investment advice.
Why do medical conference dates matter for biotech stocks?
Trial results are often presented first at medical meetings, so a company's announced presentation is a dated, public catalyst. We publish a study of how biotech stocks have historically traded into conference presentations on the research page; it is historical measurement, not a prediction, and none of this is investment advice.
