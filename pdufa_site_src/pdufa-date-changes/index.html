PDUFA date changes

Page updated November 27, 2026·next FDA decision: on the calendar·Data as of Nov 27, 2026 6:52 PM ET
31 FDA goal date changes on record, each with the date it moved from and the date it moved to, both quoted from the sponsor's documents or the FDA's own records, the reason stated, and what the FDA did next. A PDUFA date is the FDA's target for completing review, and the agency can extend it, most often by three months after a major amendment.
31 sourced extensions. 31 of them moved the goal date by about three months (89 to 92 days), the length the FDA applies after a major amendment. Ending in an FDA action: 25 (16 approved, 9 Complete Response Letters). Where an FDA record gives the action day (21), the FDA acted before the extended date 13 times, on it 5 times and after it 3 times. 6 have no FDA action on record yet. Counts of the rows below, not a prediction for any pending application.

Questions

Why do PDUFA dates change?
The FDA can extend a goal date, most often by three months, when the sponsor submits a major amendment to the application during review. Sponsors have also reported extensions for other stated reasons, such as resource constraints at the agency. A sponsor may also withdraw and resubmit, which starts a new review clock. The agency does not publish these changes as a list; each one here comes from the company's own documents.
Does an extended PDUFA date mean the FDA will reject the drug?
No such rule exists. Of the 25 extended reviews on this page that ended in an FDA action, 16 ended in approval and 9 in a Complete Response Letter. These are counts of past cases, not a prediction for any pending application.
Is this every PDUFA date change?
No. It is every change this archive has sourced, currently 31. A goal date that moved without a public announcement, or an extension known only from its new date, is not listed. We would rather show a short sourced list than imply a complete one.
Where do the dates come from?
Company press releases and SEC filings for the goal dates, quoted and linked on every row, and FDA records for the action dates where the FDA publishes them. pdufa.bio is never cited as its own source.
