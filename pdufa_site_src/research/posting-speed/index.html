How fast pdufa.bio posts FDA catalysts, to the minute

Page updated November 27, 2026·next FDA decision: on the calendar·Data as of Nov 27, 2026 6:52 PM ET
11 catalyst events on pdufa.bio have a verified time for their primary source. pdufa.bio published them a median 13 h 19 min after that source; the shortest gap was 2 h 50 min and the longest 5 d 16 h.
How each time is read. Source time: when the catalyst’s primary source was published, read on that source itself: the time a newswire page prints on the company’s release, the “Accepted” time on the SEC’s EDGAR index page of the company’s 8-K or 6-K (EDGAR prints Eastern time), or the publication time with its time zone in the FDA’s own RSS feed. When an event has more than one timed source, the earliest is used. A source that prints a date but no time is never given one. pdufa.bio time: when the change that first published the decision or the moved goal date reached the version of pdufa.bio that goes live (the site deploys within minutes of that time). Gap: the second time minus the first, in whole minutes, rounded down. An event is left out when a disclosure that prints no time may have come earlier, because the gap could then look shorter than it was.
