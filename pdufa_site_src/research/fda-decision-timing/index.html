Questions

Does the FDA always decide on the PDUFA date?
No. In this archive's 2026 sourced decisions, 13 of 18 came before the PDUFA goal date, 4 on it and 1 after. The goal date is the date by which the FDA aims to complete its review, not a fixed announcement date.
Can the FDA approve a drug early?
Yes. The PDUFA date is a target for completing the review, so the agency can act sooner, and in this archive several 2026 decisions did: the earliest by 58 days. It can also extend the date, usually when the company submits a major amendment that needs more review time.
Which decisions does this page count?
Only 2026 decisions where this archive holds the PDUFA goal date, the actual action date, and a primary source (FDA notice, SEC filing or company release) that we link.
