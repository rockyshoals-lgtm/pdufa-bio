How the entries are dated

A decision entry is published on its announcement date (or the FDA's action date when no announcement is on record). It is updated at the time the page first entered pdufa.bio's public repository, the time its "first committed" line states; pages that came in with the repository's first import (July 2026) have no such time and are dated by the announcement alone. A goal-date change is dated by the date of the change in the row's goal-date history: the day the sponsor or the FDA announced it, or the day pdufa.bio corrected the record. A CRL entry is published on the letter's date; a letter that first appears in the FDA's release after November 22, 2026 is updated on the day pdufa.bio first read it there, so a reader that sorts by updated time sees a newly released old letter as new. Dates with no time of day are written as noon Eastern. Feed times are UTC; dates on this page are Eastern.

The same data, whole, is in the API, the calendar file and the CRL data download.
