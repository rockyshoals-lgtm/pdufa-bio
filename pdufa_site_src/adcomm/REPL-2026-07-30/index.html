REPL AdComm: Jul 30, 2026

Replimune Group · Cellular, Tissue, and Gene Therapies Advisory Committee (CTGTAC).

✓ Panel voted 10-3 favorable
On Jul 30, 2026 the CTGTAC voted 10 for, 3 against that the efficacy results from the IGNYTE study are evaluable and clinically meaningful for RP1 (vusolimogene oderparepvec) in combination with nivolumab in advanced melanoma previously treated with an anti-PD-1 regimen. The vote is non-binding; the FDA's action goal date is Aug 2, 2026.
