Questions

What is an FDA advisory committee?

A panel of outside experts convened to review an application's evidence and vote on questions the FDA poses.

Is the FDA bound by the vote?

No. The vote is advisory; the FDA usually follows it but is free not to.

How many meetings are on record here?

2 advisory committee meetings are documented with their votes and outcomes as of October 6, 2026.
