FDA approvals by NDA classification code, since January 2025

Page updated November 27, 2026·next FDA decision: on the calendar·Data as of Nov 27, 2026 9:51 PM ET

Drugs@FDA lists 296 original NDA and BLA approvals with action dates from January 16, 2025 to November 24, 2026. It prints an NDA classification code (the "chemical type" in the FDA's Drugs@FDA glossary) on 251 of them. Type 1, a new molecular entity, is on 105: 47 in 2025 and 58 in 2026 (through November 24, 2026). The most common code is Type 1 - New Molecular Entity (105).

Source: openFDA drugsfda (the Drugs@FDA data), read from openFDA on 2026-11-27. The codes and their meanings are the FDA's, from its manual MAPP 5018.2, NDA Classification Codes; the meanings here are short paraphrases, and the manual is the authority. Only original approvals carry a code; efficacy supplements, such as a new indication on an existing application, do not and are not counted here (they are on /fda-approvals/drugs). An approval whose code names two types, such as Type 1 and Type 4, is listed under each, so the counts by code add up to more than the total. The manual is written for NDAs; Drugs@FDA prints the same codes on 48 original BLA approvals too. It leaves the class blank on 26 BLAs and 10 NDAs, and prints "Efficacy" as the class of 9 original approvals; those are listed as printed. Drugs@FDA covers what the FDA's drug center (CDER) reviews; vaccines and cell and gene therapies, reviewed by the biologics center, are not in it. openFDA trails Drugs@FDA by about a week, so the latest days can be missing until the next read. The FDA keeps its own list of novel drug approvals, which is a separate list; this page does not reproduce it. The FDA's manual: MAPP 5018.2, NDA Classification Codes; the FDA's own list: Novel Drug Approvals at FDA.
