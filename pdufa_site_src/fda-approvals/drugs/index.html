FDA approvals by drug application, since January 2025

Page updated November 27, 2026·next FDA decision: on the calendar·Data as of Nov 27, 2026 3:51 AM ET

Drugs@FDA lists 754 FDA approvals with action dates from January 15, 2025 to November 23, 2026, on 568 applications: 292 original approvals and 462 efficacy supplements. 108 applications have more than one approval in that time; the most is 14, on Keytruda (pembrolizumab), BLA 125514. This site's letter pages, from the FDA's CRL release as last updated 2026-11-18, include at least one Complete Response Letter on the same application number for 76 of these applications.

Source: openFDA drugsfda (the Drugs@FDA data), read from openFDA between 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-27 (each approval's read date is in the CSV's openfda_read_on column), and the FDA's released Complete Response Letters (openFDA transparency/crl, release last updated 2026-11-18) as this site's letter pages hold them. Drugs@FDA covers drugs and the therapeutic biologics the FDA's drug center (CDER) reviews; products the biologics center (CBER) reviews, such as vaccines and cell and gene therapies, are not in it. An approval here is an approved original submission of an NDA or BLA (biosimilars and medical gases included; an application approved in two original submissions counts twice) or an approved supplement in Drugs@FDA's efficacy class, which holds supplements backed by clinical data, such as a new indication. Generic approvals (ANDAs), labeling-only and manufacturing supplements are not listed. openFDA trails Drugs@FDA by about a week, so the latest days can be missing until the next read. One brand can sit on more than one application (a new formulation often has its own), and each application is listed on its own. A released CRL is matched on the application number the letter prints; it may concern a submission other than the approvals listed here, before or after them, and a letter the FDA has not released is not counted. A few approvals have no FDA letter link in Drugs@FDA; those rows link none.
