Drugs@FDA lists 758 FDA approvals with action dates from January 15, 2025 to November 24, 2026: 296 original approvals of new drug and biologic applications (286 applications) and 462 efficacy supplements. They fall under 289 sponsor-name groups; 115 groups have more than one approval.

Source: openFDA drugsfda (the Drugs@FDA data), read from openFDA between 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-27 (each approval's read date is in the CSV's openfda_read_on column). Drugs@FDA covers drugs and the therapeutic biologics the FDA's drug center (CDER) reviews; products the biologics center (CBER) reviews, such as vaccines and cell and gene therapies, are not in it. An approval here is an approved original submission of an NDA or BLA (biosimilars and medical gases included; an application approved in two original submissions counts twice) or an approved supplement in Drugs@FDA's efficacy class, which holds supplements backed by clinical data, such as a new indication. Generic approvals (ANDAs), labeling-only and manufacturing supplements are not listed. openFDA trails Drugs@FDA by about a week, so the latest days can be missing until the next read. Drugs@FDA lists each application under its current sponsor, which may not be the sponsor at approval. Approvals are grouped on the company name as openFDA prints it, with capitalization, accents, punctuation and corporate-form words (Inc., LLC, Ltd., Limited, Corp., SA, USA and the like) ignored, the rule /crl/companies uses, so one company printed two ways (an abbreviated word, another legal entity) forms two groups; each group shows every sponsor name it holds, in title case (Drugs@FDA prints them in capitals).
