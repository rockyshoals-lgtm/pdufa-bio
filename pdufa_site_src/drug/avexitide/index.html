About this program

Indication under review: Post-bariatric hypoglycaemia (PBH) after Roux-en-Y gastric bypass surgery. Sponsor: Amylyx Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (AMLX). Review status: Phase 3 LUCIDITY (n=78) met the FDA-agreed primary endpoint: a 55% reduction in the composite rate of Level 2 and Level 3 hypoglycaemic events versus placebo (p=0.000003), with all secondary endpoints met and no treatment-related serious adverse events. Avexitide holds Breakthrough Therapy and Orphan Drug designations. Amylyx has said it plans to submit an NDA to the FDA by the end of 2026; no action date exists yet, because no application has been filed.
