About this program

FDA approval tracked on pdufa.bio: spinal muscular atrophy (SMA), adults and children 2 years and older already on an SMN2-targeted treatment (approved Sep 11, 2026). Earlier approvals may not be listed here; the FDA label is the full record. Sponsor: Scholar Rock Holding Corporation (SRRK).
