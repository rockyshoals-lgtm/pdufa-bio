What the FDA decides in October 2026

Page updated October 30, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Oct 30, 2026 9:06 PM ET

Decided so far in October 2026

Ahead of its October 24 goal date, on September 11, the FDA approved Joenja (leniolisib) - paediatric APDS (Pharming Group N.V., PHAR) in activated PI3K-delta syndrome (APDS) in children (resubmission covering patients weighing 27 kg or more).

On October 2, the FDA approved Jaypirca (pirtobrutinib) - previously untreated CLL/SLL (ELI LILLY & Co, LLY) in adult patients with previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) or small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL) with no known 17p deletion.

On October 6, the FDA approved Rhapsido (remibrutinib) - symptomatic dermographism (Novartis Ag, NVS) in adults with symptomatic dermographism (SD) inadequately controlled by H1 antihistamines.

On October 8, the FDA approved Weltruza (olanzapine) extended-release (TEVA).

Ahead of its January 6 goal date, on October 14, the FDA approved ANKTIVA (N-803) + BCG - papillary-only BCG-unresponsive NMIBC (ImmunityBio, Inc., IBRX) in with BCG, adults with BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer; now includes high-grade papillary-only tumors as well as CIS with or without papillary tumors.

On October 15, the FDA approved Enspryng (satralizumab) - thyroid eye disease (Roche Holding AG, RHHBY) in adults with thyroid eye disease (TED).

Ahead of its October 17 goal date, on October 16, the FDA approved Phentolamine ophthalmic solution 0.75% (Opus Genetics, Inc., IRD) in presbyopia in adults (sNDA; RYZUMVI was already approved for pharmacologically induced mydriasis).

On October 19, within the window the sponsor had given (February 2027; no goal day was published), the FDA approved Jemperli (dostarlimab-gxly) - locally advanced rectal cancer (GSK plc, GSK) in adults with Stage II/III locally advanced rectal cancer that is microsatellite instability high (MSI-H) or mismatch repair deficient (dMMR), as determined by an FDA-authorized test (accelerated approval).

Ahead of its October 26 goal date, on October 20, the FDA approved Hibsago (bepirovirsen) - chronic hepatitis B (GSK plc, GSK) in chronic hepatitis B virus infection, in combination with nucleos(t)ide analogue therapy, in adults without cirrhosis on that therapy for at least 6 months with suppressed HBV DNA and HBsAg of 3,000 IU/mL or less (FDA-authorized test).

Questions

How many FDA decisions are scheduled for October 2026?
14 tracked PDUFA events fall in October 2026: 13 decided so far and 1 still ahead, plus 0 expected without a company-disclosed day. The FDA does not publish a forward calendar; every date here comes from a company filing or FDA notice.
Can these dates change?
Yes. A PDUFA date is the FDA's review goal, not a fixed announcement date. The agency can decide early, and it can extend the date by three months when a sponsor submits a major amendment during review.
