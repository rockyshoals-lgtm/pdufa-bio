Where the programme actually stands
Both Phase 3 trials are fully enrolled. VANQUISH-1 (obesity) completed enrollment in November 2025; VANQUISH-2 (obesity with type 2 diabetes, approximately 1,000 adults) completed enrollment in March 2026. Each evaluates subcutaneous VK2735 once weekly for 78 weeks, with the primary endpoint being percent change in body weight from baseline at week 78.
The company guides VANQUISH-2 data to the third quarter of 2026. That is company guidance, quoted as given. It is a quarter, not a date, and we do not convert it into one.
Other stated 2026 milestones. In its Q1 2026 update Viking said it expects to initiate a Phase 3 trial of oral VK2735 in 4Q26, expects data from the VK2735 maintenance dosing study in 3Q26, and had filed an IND for the amylin agonist VK3019. Quarter-end cash was stated at $603 million as of March 31, 2026.
How good was the last data, and what the market did with it

This is the part worth being precise about, because the company's characterisation and the market's reaction pointed in opposite directions, and both are facts.

What the company reported (August 19, 2025, Phase 2 VENTURE-Oral). The release is titled “Positive Top-Line Results”. The trial met its primary and secondary endpoints. Mean weight loss reached up to 12.2% (26.6 lbs) after 13 weeks versus 1.3% (2.9 lbs) for placebo. Up to 97% of treated subjects achieved at least 5% weight loss versus 10% on placebo, and up to 80% achieved at least 10% versus 5%. Weight loss was described as progressive with no plateau at 13 weeks, and 99% of GI-specific adverse events were mild or moderate.
What the stock did that day. VKTX closed down 42.1% on 64.6 million shares. A trial that met every stated endpoint was followed by the second-largest single-day move in the stock's recent history, in the opposite direction to the headline. We report both and do not attempt to adjudicate which reading was right; the release and the tape are each linked below.
