How to read this: a "surge" is a small-cap moving hard in the morning. The continuation odds estimate whether it keeps running from the open toward midday (~noon): the idea is to ride the morning wave and exit by midday, not hold all day. Odds are unbiased historical base rates; P(a name at this move × relative-volume went on to +25% intraday), built from whole-market data that includes since-delisted tickers (winners and losers). Not predictions. Pre-market: of every name that eventually surged +25%, ~83% were already up ≥10% by 9:00am.
