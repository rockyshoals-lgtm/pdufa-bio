ACHV FDA decision: Jun 20, 2026 (announced Jun 22)

Page updated November 21, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 23, 2026 1:18 AM ET
The FDA's own letter · Complete Response Letter, NDA 218995 (PDF), dated June 20, 2026, released under FDA's CRL transparency program. This page is filed under June 22, 2026.
What the letter says has to be fixed (the letter's own section headings; read against the PDF, not openFDA's OCR text):
  • Facility inspections. Following a CGMP inspection of a facility listed in the application (name redacted), FDA conveyed deficiencies to the facility; the facility's satisfactory responses to its Form 483, and possibly re-inspection and a pre-approval inspection, are required before the application can be approved.
  • Prescribing information. Submit draft labeling responsive to FDA's electronic communication of June 4, 2026, as part of the resubmission.
Clinical pharmacology (the letter says this is not an approvability issue): The proposed dosing regimen in end-stage renal disease is not adequately supported by the available pharmacokinetic data; FDA asks for modelling across a range of dialysis scenarios. The letter labels this a comment, not an approvability issue.
The letter names no clinical efficacy or safety deficiency. Postmarketing studies on pregnancy and lactation exposure would be required if the application is approved.
