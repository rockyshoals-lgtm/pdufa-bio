The FDA issued a Complete Response Letter on November 24, 2025 for AVT06, Alvotech's proposed biosimilar to Eylea (aflibercept), citing facility deficiencies. Alvotech did not announce the letter on its date.

The FDA's Complete Response Letter for BLA 761435 is dated November 24, 2025 (FDA CRL file). It cites deficiencies the FDA conveyed on a Form 483 after a pre-license inspection of a facility listed in the application, and deficiencies from a Remote Regulatory Assessment of a facility, which the FDA says may need an on-site inspection before approval. None of the five releases Alvotech filed with the SEC on Form 6-K from November 24, 2025 through January 2, 2026 announces this letter. Alvotech's annual report on Form 20-F for 2025, filed March 31, 2026, states that it received Complete Response Letters from the FDA on its BLAs for AVT03, AVT05 and AVT06 (Alvotech 20-F). On June 4, 2026 Alvotech announced that it had resubmitted the AVT06 application (Alvotech release). Because the letter was not announced on its date, this page is left out of the run-up study. No PDUFA goal date is stated, so no early or late margin is given. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.

Questions

Was AVT06 (proposed aflibercept biosimilar) approved by the FDA?

Not in this review cycle. On November 24, 2025 the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter, declining to approve the application for AVT06 (proposed aflibercept biosimilar) in its current form. A CRL is not final; the primary source is linked on this page.

Which company is behind AVT06 (proposed aflibercept biosimilar)?

AVT06 (proposed aflibercept biosimilar) is an Alvotech (ALVO) program. The FDA decision came on November 24, 2025; the company did not announce it that day; the FDA's own letter is linked on this page.
