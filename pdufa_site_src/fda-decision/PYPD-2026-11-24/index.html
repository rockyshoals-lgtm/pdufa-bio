PolyPid announced on November 24, 2026 that the FDA approved its NDA for BACFENSSI (doxycycline hyclate), an extended-release paste previously known as D-PLEX100, to reduce the occurrence of surgical site infections in adults and pediatric patients weighing 45 kg or more undergoing colorectal surgery with incision lengths greater than 20 cm (PolyPid 6-K, Exhibit 99.1). PolyPid had disclosed a PDUFA target action date of November 28, 2026 under Priority Review (PolyPid 6-K, July 29, 2026), so the announcement came four days before it. The FDA's own record of the action day had not been read when this page was published, so no early or late margin is given yet. Per PolyPid, in SHIELD II the primary endpoint event occurred in 10.9% of patients on BACFENSSI plus standard of care and 18.1% on standard of care alone (p=0.004).
