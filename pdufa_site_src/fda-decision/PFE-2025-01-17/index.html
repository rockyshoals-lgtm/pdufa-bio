The FDA's Complete Response Letter for NDA 219112 is dated January 17, 2025 (FDA CRL file). It is addressed to Hospira, Inc. in Lake Forest, Illinois and names Diazepam Injection. It is the first action on the application, and its deficiencies fall under product quality and nonclinical headings, with the details redacted; it also asks for revised carton and container labeling. Hospira has been part of Pfizer since Pfizer completed its acquisition on September 3, 2015 (Pfizer release). None of Pfizer's press releases from January 17 through February 10, 2025 announces the letter. Because the letter was not announced on its date, this page is left out of the run-up study. No PDUFA goal date is stated, so no early or late margin is given. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.
