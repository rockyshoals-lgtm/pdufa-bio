Pluvicto FDA approval for hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer: Jul 31, 2026

Page updated November 20, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 20, 2026 5:50 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

Pluvicto (lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan) was approved by the FDA on July 31, 2026 for with an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor, adults with PSMA-positive metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive prostate cancer.

Verified against: FDA action day July 31, 2026 (NDA 215833 SUPPL-37 (FDA letter)) · Goal date: none on record.

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository on Nov 1, 2026, 9:41 PM ET (public commit c48b6e7). Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA approved Pluvicto in combination with an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor for PSMA-positive metastatic prostate cancer that is androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive, extending its use to an earlier stage of the disease.
