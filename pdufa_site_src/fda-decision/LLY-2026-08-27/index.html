Mounjaro (tirzepatide) FDA approval for cardiovascular risk reduction: Aug 27, 2026

Page updated November 20, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 20, 2026 7:17 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

Mounjaro (tirzepatide) was approved by the FDA for reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events in adults with type 2 diabetes and established cardiovascular disease (SURPASS-CVOT); the approval was announced on August 27, 2026.

Verified against: FDA record: FDA approval letter, NDA 215866 S-044/S-045 · Goal date: none on record.

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository on Nov 1, 2026, 9:41 PM ET (public commit c48b6e7). Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA approved a new Mounjaro indication to reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events, based on the SURPASS-CVOT trial comparing tirzepatide with dulaglutide.

The FDA's letter for NDA 215866 supplements 44 and 45 is dated August 27, 2026 (FDA letter). It approves updates to the prescribing information based on trial I8F-MC-GPGN (SURPASS-CVOT), the effect of tirzepatide versus dulaglutide on major adverse cardiovascular events in patients with type 2 diabetes, including a new indication to reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction or non-fatal stroke). Lilly stated no goal date for this supplement, so no early or late margin is published.
