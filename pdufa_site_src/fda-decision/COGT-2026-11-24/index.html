Ekitna (bezuclastinib) FDA approval for GIST, with sunitinib: Nov 24, 2026

✓ APPROVED

The FDA approved Ekitna (bezuclastinib) with sunitinib on November 24, 2026 for adults with advanced GIST previously treated with imatinib, on the PEAK trial, 6 days before its November 30 goal date.

The FDA's notification states that on November 24, 2026 it approved bezuclastinib (Ekitna, Cogent Biosciences, Inc) in combination with sunitinib for adults with advanced gastrointestinal stromal tumors who have received prior treatment with imatinib (FDA, November 24, 2026). Cogent had disclosed a PDUFA goal date of November 30, 2026 for GIST (Cogent 8-K, August 10, 2026); the FDA acted 6 days early. The FDA's letter had not been posted on Drugs@FDA when this page was published. Per the FDA, in PEAK (NCT05208047), 413 patients, median progression-free survival was 16.5 months with bezuclastinib plus sunitinib and 9.2 months with sunitinib alone (hazard ratio 0.50, 95% CI 0.39 to 0.65); overall survival data were not mature. Cogent's systemic mastocytosis applications are separate and remain open.
