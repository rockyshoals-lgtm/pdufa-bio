neffy (epinephrine nasal spray) FDA approval, age criterion removed: Mar 26, 2026

Page updated October 24, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Oct 26, 2026 12:56 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

neffy (epinephrine nasal spray) was approved by the FDA on March 26, 2026 for emergency treatment of type I allergic reactions including anaphylaxis in adults and pediatric patients weighing 15 kg or more.

The FDA approved a labeling change for neffy (epinephrine nasal spray) on March 26, 2026 that removes the age criterion, so the indication now covers adults and pediatric patients weighing 15 kg (33 lb) or more (NDA 214697, supplements 2 and 4). ARS Pharmaceuticals announced it on March 27, 2026.
