The FDA's released letter on BLA 761451 (anifrolumab) is dated October 10, 2025 (the letter's text). It states that an amendment with a corrected interim dataset for study D3465C00001, submitted late in the review cycle, identifies critical data quality issues that affect key analyses, including the primary endpoint, and that the corrected data can be submitted for a new review cycle. AstraZeneca's release of February 3, 2026 says the FDA issued a CRL and that the company has since provided the information the CRL requested; it does not state when the letter was received (AstraZeneca, February 3, 2026). This page is filed under the day AstraZeneca disclosed the letter, 116 days after the letter's date. The FDA later approved BLA 761451, Saphnelo for subcutaneous use, on April 24, 2026 (that decision). A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.
