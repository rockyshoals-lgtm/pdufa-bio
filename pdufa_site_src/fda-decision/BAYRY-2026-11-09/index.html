Sunharbo (asundexian) FDA approval: Nov 9, 2026

Page updated November 11, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 12, 2026 2:24 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

Sunharbo (asundexian) was approved by the FDA on November 9, 2026 for secondary stroke prevention, the 48th novel drug approval of 2026 on the FDA's Novel Drug Approvals list.

Verified against: FDA action day November 9, 2026 (FDA press announcement) · Announcement November 9, 2026 (FDA press announcement) · Goal date none sourced.

The FDA approved Sunharbo (asundexian) 50 mg tablets on November 9, 2026, the first factor XIa inhibitor, for use with antiplatelet therapy to reduce the risk of stroke in adults after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or a high-risk transient ischemic attack.

Per the FDA: OCEANIC-STROKE enrolled 12,327 adults; Sunharbo reduced the risk of ischemic stroke by 26% versus placebo over a median 14.2 months; major bleeding was similar overall but higher in patients over 75 and in the first 90 days. Sunharbo is not approved for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation.
