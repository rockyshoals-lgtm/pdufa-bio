Exdensur (depemokimab) FDA approval for severe eosinophilic asthma: Dec 16, 2025

✓ APPROVED

Exdensur (depemokimab) was approved by the FDA on December 16, 2025 for add-on maintenance treatment of severe asthma with an eosinophilic phenotype, ages 12 and older.

Verified against: FDA action day December 16, 2025 (BLA 761458 Original 1 (Drugs@FDA)) · Goal date: none on record.

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository on Nov 26, 2026, 1:09 AM ET (public commit c6f78c3). Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA approved Exdensur (depemokimab) on December 16, 2025 as add-on maintenance treatment of severe asthma with an eosinophilic phenotype in patients aged 12 and older, on its PDUFA goal date. The same day it issued a Complete Response Letter for the nasal polyps indication in the same application.

Drugs@FDA lists the approval of BLA 761458 (Original 1) with an action date of December 16, 2025 (Drugs@FDA); the posted approval letter covers add-on maintenance treatment of severe asthma characterized by an eosinophilic phenotype in adult and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older, and its electronic signature is dated December 17, 2025 (FDA letter). The FDA's Complete Response Letter for BLA 761458 Original 2, chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps, is dated December 16, 2025 (CRL letter). GSK announced the asthma approval on December 16, 2025 (GSK's announcement), reporting 58% and 48% reductions in the rate of annualised asthma exacerbations in the SWIFT-1 and SWIFT-2 trials. GSK had stated a PDUFA date of December 16, 2025 when the applications were accepted (GSK 6-K, March 3, 2025).
