The FDA issued a Complete Response Letter on February 26, 2026 for AMP-004, Amphastar's proposed biosimilar to NovoLog (insulin aspart), finding its pharmacokinetic data unreliable. Amphastar did not announce the letter on its date.

The FDA's Complete Response Letter for BLA 761403 is dated February 26, 2026 (FDA CRL file). The letter refers to the product as I004. The FDA writes that, because of deficiencies in bioanalytical method validation and bioanalysis for insulin aspart noted on a Form 483, the pharmacokinetic data from Study API-I004-CL-B are unreliable and cannot support a finding of no clinically meaningful differences from US-licensed NovoLog, and it recommends a new pharmacokinetic study with a validated method. The letter also cites deficiencies from a pre-license inspection of a facility listed in the application. Amphastar's fourth-quarter results release the same day says it has one biosimilar insulin candidate filed with the FDA (Amphastar release, Feb 26, 2026), and its first-quarter release of May 7, 2026 says the same and does not mention a Complete Response Letter (Amphastar release, May 7, 2026). On its earnings calls on February 26 and May 7, 2026, per the call transcripts, Amphastar described the insulin aspart application (AMP-004) as still in progress, with a commercial launch planned for 2027. Because the letter was not announced on its date, this page is left out of the run-up study. No PDUFA goal date is stated, so no early or late margin is given. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.
