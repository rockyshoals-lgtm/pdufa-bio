Talzenna (talazoparib) with Xtandi FDA approval for HRR-mutated mCSPC: Oct 26, 2026

✓ APPROVED

The FDA approved Talzenna (talazoparib) with Xtandi (enzalutamide) on October 26, 2026 for HRR gene-mutated metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer, on TALAPRO-3.

The FDA's notification states that on October 26, 2026 it approved talazoparib (Talzenna, Pfizer Inc.) with enzalutamide for adults with metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive prostate cancer with HRR gene mutations detected by an FDA-authorized test (FDA, October 26, 2026). Pfizer announced the approval at 1:41 PM Eastern the same day (Pfizer's announcement). Pfizer had said the PDUFA action date fell in the last quarter of 2026 (Pfizer, July 22, 2026), a quarter rather than a day, so no early or late margin is given. Per the FDA, TALAPRO-3 (NCT04821622) randomized 599 patients; adding talazoparib to enzalutamide gave a radiographic progression-free survival hazard ratio of 0.48 (95% CI 0.36 to 0.65), with the median not reached versus 45.8 months. The application had Priority Review and was reviewed under Project Orbis.
