Capricor announced on the evening of November 20, 2026 (7:55 PM ET) that it received a Complete Response Letter for its resubmitted BLA for deramiocel, its cell therapy for Duchenne muscular dystrophy (Capricor's announcement). Per Capricor, the letter states that the FDA does not consider the data sufficient to establish substantial evidence of effectiveness and cites certain other clinical deficiencies; Capricor said it is reviewing the letter and will share next steps after its evaluation. The PDUFA goal date was November 22, 2026, moved from August 22 after a major amendment to the application (Capricor, August 24, 2026). The release does not state the letter's own date, so no early or late margin is given. This is deramiocel's second Complete Response Letter; the first was announced on July 11, 2025 (CAPR, July 11, 2025). A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.
