Tukysa (tucatinib) FDA approval for HER2+ breast cancer maintenance: Oct 7, 2026

Page updated November 18, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 19, 2026 12:24 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

Tukysa (tucatinib) + trastuzumab + pertuzumab - HER2+ metastatic breast cancer maintenance (HER2CLIMB-05) was approved by the FDA on October 7, 2026 for maintenance treatment, in combination with trastuzumab and pertuzumab, of adults with unresectable locally advanced or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer following induction therapy.

Verified against: FDA action day October 7, 2026 (NDA 213411 SUPPL-10 (FDA letter)) · Goal date: none on record.
