AVT05 golimumab biosimilar (ALVO) FDA Complete Response Letter: Nov 26, 2025

This page entered pdufa.bio's records by Nov 26, 2026, 3:48 PM ET. Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA issued a Complete Response Letter on November 26, 2025 for BLA 761461, a second Alvotech application for AVT05, its proposed biosimilar to Simponi (golimumab), citing facility inspection deficiencies. Alvotech did not announce the letter on its date.

The FDA's Complete Response Letter for BLA 761461 is dated November 26, 2025 and names the product as AVT05 (FDA CRL file). It cites deficiencies the FDA conveyed on a Form 483 after a pre-license inspection of a facility listed in the application, and asks for a Medication Guide statement on the carton and container labeling. The FDA's letter on Alvotech's other AVT05 application, BLA 761462, is dated October 31, 2025 and has its own page (ALVO, Oct 31, 2025 letter). None of the five releases Alvotech filed with the SEC on Form 6-K from November 24, 2025 through January 2, 2026 announces this letter. Alvotech's annual report on Form 20-F for 2025, filed March 31, 2026, states that it received Complete Response Letters from the FDA on its BLAs for AVT03, AVT05 and AVT06 (Alvotech 20-F). On June 4, 2026 Alvotech announced that it had resubmitted its AVT05 applications for Simponi and Simponi Aria (Alvotech release). Because the letter was not announced on its date, this page is left out of the run-up study. No PDUFA goal date is stated, so no early or late margin is given. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.

Questions

Was AVT05 (proposed golimumab biosimilar), BLA 761461 approved by the FDA?

Not in this review cycle. On November 26, 2025 the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter, declining to approve the application for AVT05 (proposed golimumab biosimilar), BLA 761461 in its current form. A CRL is not final; the primary source is linked on this page.

Which company is behind AVT05 (proposed golimumab biosimilar), BLA 761461?
