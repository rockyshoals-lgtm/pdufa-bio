Olumiant (baricitinib) FDA approval for adolescents with severe alopecia areata: Sep 25, 2026

Page updated October 24, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Oct 25, 2026 12:07 PM ET

Olumiant (baricitinib) was approved by the FDA on September 25, 2026 for severe alopecia areata in pediatric patients 12 years of age and older (previously adults only).

The FDA approved Olumiant (baricitinib) for severe alopecia areata in patients 12 years of age and older on September 25, 2026 (NDA 207924, supplement 9), extending the 2022 adult approval to adolescents. Lilly announced it the same day.

The FDA's approval letter for NDA 207924 supplement 9 is dated September 25, 2026 and provides for the treatment of severe alopecia areata in adults and pediatric patients 12 years of age and older (FDA approval letter). Lilly announced the approval the same day (Lilly release). Per Lilly, the approval rests on 36-week data from the adolescent cohort of the Phase 3 BRAVE-AA-PEDS study (median baseline SALT score 100, complete scalp hair loss): 42% of patients on 4 mg and 27% on 2 mg reached 80% or more scalp hair coverage (SALT 20 or less), compared with 5% on placebo. pdufa.bio did not hold a goal date for this supplement, so no early or late margin is computed.
