Repatha (evolocumab) FDA approval, VESALIUS-CV label expansion: Oct 9, 2026

Page updated November 18, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 19, 2026 4:43 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

Repatha (evolocumab) - cardiovascular risk reduction label expansion (VESALIUS-CV) was approved by the FDA on October 9, 2026 for to reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, stroke, unstable angina requiring hospitalization, coronary revascularization, or any ischemia-driven arterial revascularization) in adults at increased risk for these events; label now includes the VESALIUS-CV trial in patients without a prior myocardial infarction or stroke.

Verified against: FDA action day October 9, 2026 (BLA 125522 SUPPL-47 (Drugs@FDA)) · Goal date: none on record.

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository on Oct 15, 2026, 9:39 PM ET (public commit da583b8). Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA approved an efficacy supplement for Repatha (evolocumab) on October 9, 2026 that revises the cardiovascular risk-reduction indication and adds the VESALIUS-CV trial, in adults without a prior heart attack or stroke, to the label (BLA 125522, supplement 47). As of October 14, 2026 Amgen had not issued a release on it.
