OTC NARCAN Nasal Spray FDA approval of 6-count and 24-count multipacks: Feb 11, 2026

Page updated October 17, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Oct 19, 2026 12:50 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

OTC NARCAN Nasal Spray - 6-count and 24-count multipacks was approved by the FDA for emergency treatment of known or suspected opioid overdose (over-the-counter naloxone 4 mg nasal spray), new 6-count and 24-count packaging; the approval was announced on February 12, 2026.

The FDA approved a packaging supplement for over-the-counter NARCAN Nasal Spray adding 6-count and 24-count multipacks on February 11, 2026 (NDA 208411, supplement 11). Emergent BioSolutions announced it on February 12, 2026.
