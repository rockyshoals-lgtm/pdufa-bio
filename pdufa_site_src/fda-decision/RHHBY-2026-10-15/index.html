Enspryng (satralizumab) FDA approval for thyroid eye disease: Oct 15, 2026

Page updated October 24, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Oct 28, 2026 2:26 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

Enspryng (satralizumab) was approved by the FDA on October 15, 2026 for adults with thyroid eye disease (TED).

Verified against: FDA action day October 15, 2026 (BLA 761149 S-008) · Announcement October 15, 2026 (Genentech press release); also Roche media release, Basel, dated October 16, 2026 · Goal date October 15, 2026 (Genentech press release).

Genentech says Enspryng is the first and only at-home subcutaneous treatment for thyroid eye disease, given every four weeks during maintenance. It is the second indication for Enspryng, an IL-6 receptor inhibitor, after neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder in 2020.

Genentech announced on October 15, 2026 that the FDA had approved Enspryng for adults with thyroid eye disease (Genentech's announcement). The goal date was October 15, 2026, under priority review (Genentech, June 29, 2026). Drugs@FDA now records the FDA's approval letter for BLA 761149 supplement 8 dated October 15, 2026 (FDA letter), the goal date, so the decision came on its goal date; this page carried the announcement day alone until the letter was posted. Roche's own release of the same approval is datelined Basel, October 16, 2026 (Roche release), which is why October 15 and October 16 both circulate as the approval date; both are announcement dates. The approval rests on the Phase III SatraGO-1 (NCT05987423) and SatraGO-2 (NCT06106828) studies, identically designed and placebo-controlled, 258 patients in 19 countries. Per Genentech, SatraGO-1 did not meet its pre-specified primary endpoint at week 24; SatraGO-2 showed a proptosis response of 52.9% with Enspryng versus 23.4% on placebo and diplopia reduction of 60.7% versus 25.8% in patients with active TED, with consistent results in the overall population. Genentech reported no new safety signals. A further Enspryng decision is pending: MOGAD, with a goal date of January 10, 2027 (Enspryng MOGAD PDUFA date).
