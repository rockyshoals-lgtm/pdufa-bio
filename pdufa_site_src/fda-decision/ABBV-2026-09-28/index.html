JUVMO (tavapadon) was approved by the FDA on September 25, 2026 for Parkinson's disease in adults, the 43rd novel drug approval of 2026 on the FDA's Novel Drug Approvals list.

Verified against: FDA action day September 25, 2026 (NDA 220415 ORIG-1) · Announcement September 28, 2026 (FDA approval letter, NDA 220415) · Goal date: none on record.

This page entered pdufa.bio's records on Oct 3, 2026, 3:28 PM ET. Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

AbbVie announced the approval on September 28, 2026 and describes JUVMO as the first selective D1/D5 receptor agonist approved for adults with Parkinson's disease.
