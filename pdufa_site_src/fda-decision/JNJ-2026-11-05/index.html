RYBREVANT FASPRO FDA approval for head and neck cancer: Nov 5, 2026

Page updated November 20, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 22, 2026 9:27 PM ET

RYBREVANT FASPRO (amivantamab and hyaluronidase-lpuj) was approved by the FDA on November 5, 2026 for adults with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma that progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody (accelerated approval).

The FDA's accelerated approval letter for BLA 761433 supplement 2, signed November 5, 2026 (FDA letter), covers RYBREVANT FASPRO as a single agent for adults with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC) whose disease has progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody. The letter states that it is not indicated for HPV-associated oropharyngeal squamous cell carcinoma, and that the supplemental application was received on May 20, 2026. Johnson & Johnson announced the approval the same evening and reported a 42 percent overall response rate (95 percent confidence interval, 32 to 52 percent) (Johnson & Johnson, November 5, 2026). We hold no publicly disclosed PDUFA goal date for this supplement, so no early or late margin is given.

Questions

Was RYBREVANT FASPRO approved for head and neck cancer?
