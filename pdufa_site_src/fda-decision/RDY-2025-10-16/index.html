DRL_RI rituximab biosimilar (RDY) FDA Complete Response Letter: Oct 16, 2025

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository by Nov 26, 2026, 5:17 PM ET. Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA issued a second Complete Response Letter on October 16, 2025 for DRL_RI, Dr. Reddy's proposed biosimilar to Rituxan (rituximab), citing deficiencies from a pre-license facility inspection. Dr. Reddy's did not announce the letter on its date.

The FDA's Complete Response Letter for BLA 761376 is dated October 16, 2025 and names the product as DRL_RI (FDA CRL file). It is addressed to Dr. Reddy's Laboratories SA in care of Parexel International. The letter says the application was a complete response to the FDA's April 19, 2024 action letter (that letter's text), and that after a pre-license inspection of a facility listed in the application the FDA conveyed deficiencies on a Form 483 that the facility should answer before the company responds; product quality deficiencies are redacted. Dr. Reddy's filed no release with the SEC on Form 6-K from October 14 through October 24, 2025 that announces this letter, and its second-quarter results filed October 24, 2025 do not mention it. The FDA approved BLA 761376 on July 31, 2026 as Zimrixby (rituximab-cdxx), and its approval letter says the resubmission of February 2, 2026 responded to the October 16, 2025 action letter (FDA approval letter). Because the letter was not announced on its date, this page is left out of the run-up study. No PDUFA goal date is stated, so no early or late margin is given. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.

Questions

Was DRL_RI (proposed rituximab biosimilar) approved by the FDA?

Not in this review cycle. On October 16, 2025 the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter, declining to approve the application for DRL_RI (proposed rituximab biosimilar) in its current form. A CRL is not final; the primary source is linked on this page.

Which company is behind DRL_RI (proposed rituximab biosimilar)?

DRL_RI (proposed rituximab biosimilar) is a Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Limited (RDY) program. The FDA decision came on October 16, 2025; the company did not announce it that day; the FDA's own letter is linked on this page.
