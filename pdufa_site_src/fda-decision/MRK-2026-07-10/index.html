MRK FDA decision: Jul 10, 2026

KEYTRUDA (pembrolizumab) plus Padcev (enfortumab vedotin-ejfv) was approved by the FDA for muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC); the approval was announced on July 10, 2026.

Verified against: No FDA record of the action day on this page · Announcement July 10, 2026 (Merck press release) · Goal date: none on record.
