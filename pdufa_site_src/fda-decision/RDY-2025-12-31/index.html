Dr. Reddy's stock exchange disclosure dated December 31, 2025, filed with the SEC on Form 6-K that day, says its subsidiary Dr. Reddy's Laboratories SA received a Complete Response Letter from the FDA for its BLA for AVT03 (denosumab), and that the CRL refers to the observations from a pre-license inspection of Alvotech's Reykjavik manufacturing facility (Dr. Reddy's disclosure, SEC Exhibit 99.1). The FDA's released letter on BLA 761471 is dated December 30, 2025 and names the product as AVT03 (the letter's text). It says the FDA conveyed deficiencies on a Form 483 after a pre-license inspection of a facility listed in the application, asks for revised labeling, and agrees that a REMS will be needed for AVT03 if it is approved, because of the risk of severe hypocalcemia in patients with advanced chronic kidney disease. Alvotech's annual report on Form 20-F for 2025 states that the FDA issued Complete Response Letters on the BLAs for AVT03, AVT05 and AVT06 (Alvotech 20-F). The disclosure states no PDUFA goal date, so no early or late margin is given. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.

Questions

Was AVT03 (proposed denosumab biosimilar) approved by the FDA?

Not in this review cycle. On December 30, 2025 the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter, declining to approve the application for AVT03 (proposed denosumab biosimilar) in its current form. The company announced it on December 31, 2025. A CRL is not final; the primary source is linked on this page.

Which company is behind AVT03 (proposed denosumab biosimilar)?

AVT03 (proposed denosumab biosimilar) is a Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Limited (RDY) program. The FDA decision came on December 30, 2025; the FDA's own record of the decision is linked on this page.
