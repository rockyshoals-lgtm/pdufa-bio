Wilserdly (giredestrant) FDA approval for adjuvant early breast cancer: Nov 23, 2026

Page updated November 27, 2026·next FDA decision: on the calendar·Data as of Nov 27, 2026 8:36 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

Wilserdly (Giredestrant (lidERA)) was approved by the FDA on November 23, 2026 for adjuvant treatment of adults with ER-positive, HER2-negative, stage I, II and III breast cancer at intermediate to high risk of recurrence, as a single agent, the 51st novel drug approval of 2026 on the FDA's Novel Drug Approvals list.

The FDA's notification states that on November 23, 2026 it approved giredestrant (Wilserdly, Genentech, Inc.) as a single agent for the adjuvant treatment of adults with ER-positive, HER2-negative, stage I, II and III breast cancer at intermediate to high risk of recurrence (FDA, November 23, 2026). Genentech announced it the same day (Genentech's announcement). Genentech had said the FDA was expected to decide by November 30, 2026 (Genentech, June 1, 2026); the FDA acted 7 days early. Per the FDA, in lidERA (NCT04961996), 4,170 patients, the 36-month invasive disease-free survival rate was 92.4% with giredestrant and 89.6% with standard endocrine therapy (hazard ratio 0.70, 95% CI 0.57 to 0.87). The use with everolimus in ESR1-mutated advanced breast cancer was approved separately on November 16, 2026.

Questions

Was giredestrant approved for early breast cancer?

Yes. The FDA approved giredestrant (Wilserdly) on November 23, 2026 as a single agent for the adjuvant treatment of adults with ER-positive, HER2-negative, stage I, II and III breast cancer at intermediate to high risk of recurrence, per the FDA's own notification. Genentech announced it the same day.

Which company is behind Wilserdly (giredestrant)?

Wilserdly (giredestrant) is a Roche (Genentech) (RHHBY) program. The FDA decision came on November 23, 2026; the company's own announcement is linked on this page.

How did RHHBY stock react to the FDA decision?

RHHBY moved +1.1%, measured as the 2026-11-23 close against the 2026-11-20 close. It is one trading day's move, so it can include news other than the decision. Historical measurement, not a prediction, and not investment advice.

Was the adjuvant Wilserdly decision early?

Yes, by 7 days. The FDA acted on November 23, 2026; Genentech had said on June 1, 2026 that the FDA was expected to decide by November 30, 2026.

Is this the same approval as Wilserdly with everolimus?

No. The FDA approved Wilserdly with everolimus for ESR1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer on November 16, 2026. The November 23 approval is the separate adjuvant use, on the lidERA trial.
