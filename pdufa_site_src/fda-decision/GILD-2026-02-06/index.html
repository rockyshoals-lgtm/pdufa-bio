Yescarta (axicabtagene ciloleucel) FDA label update, primary CNS lymphoma: Feb 5, 2026

Page updated November 20, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 23, 2026 12:29 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

Yescarta (axicabtagene ciloleucel) was approved by the FDA for a label update removing the limitation of use for primary central nervous system lymphoma (no new indication); the approval was announced on February 6, 2026.

Verified against: FDA record: FDA letter, BLA 125643 supplement 753 · Announcement February 6, 2026 · Goal date: none on record.

This page has been in pdufa.bio's public repository since the repository's first import on Jul 26, 2026, 12:07 AM ET (public commit 07d961e), so the repository does not show when we first recorded this decision. Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA approved a labeling supplement for Yescarta (axicabtagene ciloleucel) on February 5, 2026 that removes the Limitations of Use statement for primary central nervous system lymphoma (BLA 125643, supplement 753). Kite, a Gilead company, announced it on February 6, 2026.
