DUPIXENT (dupilumab) was approved by the FDA on June 18, 2025 for adults with bullous pemphigoid.

Verified against: FDA action day June 18, 2025 (BLA 761055 S-070 (FDA letter)) · Announcement June 20, 2025 · Goal date: none on record.
