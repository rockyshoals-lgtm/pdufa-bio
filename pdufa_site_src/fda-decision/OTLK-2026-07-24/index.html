OTLK FDA decision: Jul 24, 2026

Page updated November 18, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 23, 2026 4:09 AM ET
✓ APPROVED

bevacizumab was approved by the FDA on July 24, 2026 for neovascular Age-related Macular Degeneration.

Verified against: FDA action day July 24, 2026 (BLA 761320 ORIG-1) · Announcement July 24, 2026 (sponsor announcement) · Goal date July 29, 2026 (Outlook Therapeutics 8-K).

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository on Jul 27, 2026, 12:21 AM ET (public commit 39c6a7a). Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

LYTENAVA (bevacizumab-vikg), also known as ONS-5010, approved as the first and only FDA-approved ophthalmic formulation of bevacizumab for the treatment of neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (nAMD) (Jul 24 2026). The approval followed a resubmitted BLA (Class 1 review) after a successful appeal of the December 2025 Complete Response Letter; the FDA acted ahead of the July 29 2026 PDUFA target date.
