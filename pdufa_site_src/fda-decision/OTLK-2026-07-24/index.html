OTLK FDA decision — Jul 24, 2026

✓ APPROVED

LYTENAVA (bevacizumab-vikg), also known as ONS-5010, approved as the first and only FDA-approved ophthalmic formulation of bevacizumab for the treatment of neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (nAMD) (Jul 24 2026). The approval followed a resubmitted BLA (Class 1 review) after a successful appeal of the December 2025 Complete Response Letter; the FDA acted ahead of the July 29 2026 PDUFA target date.
