The PDUFA target action date was September 18, 2026; the FDA approved zidesamtinib on July 22, nearly two months early. Efficacy came from ARROS-1, a multicenter, single-arm, open-label, multi-cohort trial in 117 patients: 59 who had received one prior ROS1 TKI and 58 who had received two or more, including lorlatinib, repotrectinib and/or taletrectinib. Zidesamtinib held Breakthrough Therapy and Orphan Drug designations. Nuvalent is partnered with GSK, which announced the approval alongside the FDA notice.
