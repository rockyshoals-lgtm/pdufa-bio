Pimjetmi (pimicotinib) was approved by the FDA on November 10, 2026 for tenosynovial giant cell tumor.

Verified against: FDA action day November 10, 2026 (FDA oncology approval notification) · Announcement November 10, 2026 (FDA oncology approval notification) · Goal date none sourced.

The FDA approved Pimjetmi (pimicotinib), EMD Serono's kinase inhibitor, on November 10, 2026 for adults with symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumor for which surgical resection may cause worsening functional limitation or severe morbidity.
