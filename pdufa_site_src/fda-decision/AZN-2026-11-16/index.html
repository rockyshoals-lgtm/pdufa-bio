The FDA's approval letter for BLA 761488, signed November 13, 2026 (FDA letter), covers Klygefa (gefurulimab-gaks) for adults with generalized myasthenia gravis who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody positive. The letter states that the application was received on November 13, 2025, and it approves a REMS because of the risk of serious meningococcal infections. AstraZeneca announced the approval on November 16, 2026 and describes once-weekly subcutaneous self-administration by autoinjector (AstraZeneca, November 16, 2026). Per AstraZeneca, in the Phase III PREVAIL trial (260 patients) the MG-ADL total score improved more than with placebo at week 26 (treatment difference -1.6, p<0.0001). We hold no publicly disclosed PDUFA goal date for this application, so no early or late margin is given.
