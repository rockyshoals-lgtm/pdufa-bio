The PDUFA target action date was August 25, 2026 and the FDA approved the supplemental BLA that day. Zanidatamab originated at Zymeworks and is partnered to Jazz Pharmaceuticals, which holds the US application; this listing exists because the approval is material to Zymeworks, which is entitled to a $250 million milestone payment on it. The approval covers all HER2-positive patients (IHC 3+ and IHC 2+/ISH+) regardless of PD-L1 status, based on the Phase 3 HERIZON-GEA-01 trial: median progression-free survival of 12.4 months against 8.1 months for trastuzumab plus chemotherapy, a 35% reduction in the risk of progression or death.
