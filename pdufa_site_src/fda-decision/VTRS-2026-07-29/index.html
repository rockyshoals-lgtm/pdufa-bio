VTRS FDA decision — Jul 29, 2026

✓ APPROVED

Gwyn Lo (norelgestromin and ethinyl estradiol transdermal system), Viatris' internal candidate MR-100A-01, approved as a new low-dose estrogen combined hormonal contraceptive (CHC) patch worn once weekly (Jul 29 2026). The FDA acted one day ahead of the July 30 2026 PDUFA target date. Approval was granted under the 505(b)(2) pathway and supported by the Phase 3 Luminous Study (NCT05139121); the label reflects demonstrated efficacy for women of childbearing potential with a BMI below 30 kg/m².
