Nelkarmi (neladalkib) FDA accelerated approval for ALK-positive NSCLC: Nov 17, 2026

Page updated November 17, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 17, 2026 5:13 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

Nelkarmi (Neladalkib (NVL-655)) was approved by the FDA on November 17, 2026 for adults with locally advanced or metastatic ALK-positive NSCLC previously treated with one prior ALK TKI consisting of alectinib, or with two or more prior ALK TKIs and platinum-based chemotherapy (accelerated approval).

Verified against: FDA action day November 17, 2026 (FDA accelerated approval notification) · Announcement November 17, 2026 (GSK plc press release) · Goal date November 27, 2026 (Nuvalent press release).

This page was first committed to pdufa.bio's public repository on Nov 17, 2026, 4:20 PM ET (public commit b552701). Dates on this page are the FDA's and the sponsor's; this line is ours.

The FDA granted accelerated approval to Nelkarmi (neladalkib) on November 17, 2026 for adults with previously treated locally advanced or metastatic ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer, on the single-arm ALKOVE-1 study, 10 days before its November 27 goal date.

The FDA's notification states that on November 17, 2026 it granted accelerated approval to neladalkib (Nelkarmi, Nuvalent, Inc.) for adults with locally advanced or metastatic ALK-positive NSCLC who previously received one prior ALK TKI consisting of alectinib, or two or more prior ALK TKIs and platinum-based chemotherapy (FDA, November 17, 2026). GSK, which acquired Nuvalent in July 2026, announced the approval the same day from London (GSK's announcement). The PDUFA goal date was November 27, 2026, assigned when the NDA was accepted with Priority Review (Nuvalent, May 27, 2026); the FDA acted 10 days early. The FDA's letter had not been posted on Drugs@FDA when this page was published. Per the FDA, in ALKOVE-1 (NCT05384626) confirmed overall response by blinded independent central review was 49% (95% CI 37 to 62) in 67 patients after alectinib and 32% (95% CI 24 to 40) in 155 patients after two or more ALK TKIs and platinum-based chemotherapy; 52% and 51% of responders had a response lasting at least 12 months. Neladalkib holds Breakthrough Therapy and Orphan Drug designations; continued approval may be contingent on a confirmatory trial.
