RHHBY FDA decision: Nov 13, 2026

Page updated November 13, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 13, 2026 11:32 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

On November 13, 2026, the FDA announced its approval of Gazyva (obinutuzumab) - primary membranous nephropathy (MAJESTY) for primary membranous nephropathy.

Verified against: FDA action day not yet posted on Drugs@FDA · Announcement November 13, 2026 (FDA notice) · Goal month November 2026, no day stated (Genentech press release).

The FDA approved Gazyva (obinutuzumab) for adults with primary membranous nephropathy at risk of disease progression, to induce remission of proteinuria, per its notice of November 13, 2026: the first drug approved specifically for the disease.
