Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa) FDA approval for papillary-only NMIBC: Oct 14, 2026

Page updated October 24, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Oct 29, 2026 2:28 PM ET
✓ APPROVED

ANKTIVA (N-803) + BCG was approved by the FDA on October 14, 2026 for BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer with papillary-only tumors.

Verified against: FDA action day October 14, 2026 (FDA approval notification) · Announcement October 14, 2026 (ImmunityBio 8-K exhibit 99.1) · Goal date January 6, 2027 (ImmunityBio, Inc. 8-K).

The FDA approved an expanded indication for Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln) with BCG on October 14, 2026, adding adults with BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer with high-grade papillary-only tumors to the existing CIS indication. The goal date was January 6, 2027, so the action came 84 days early. ImmunityBio announced the approval the same day.

The FDA's approval notification is dated October 14, 2026 and states the approval was made that day; it revises the 2024 indication (BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with CIS, with or without papillary tumors) to include patients with high-grade papillary-only tumors (FDA approval notification). The goal date was January 6, 2027: ImmunityBio's 8-K exhibit of August 4, 2026 says the FDA accepted the sBLA "for ANKTIVA plus BCG in patients with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with papillary disease without CIS" and assigned that PDUFA date (ImmunityBio 8-K exhibit, August 4, 2026). Both dates come from documents that state them, so the action came 84 days before the goal date. Per the FDA, efficacy was previously shown in Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 (NCT03022825), 77 patients with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with CIS: complete response rate 62% (95% CI 51 to 73), with 58% of responders lasting 12 months or more. The FDA extended that result to papillary-only disease on review of the available scientific information, citing the overlapping clinical features of the two tumor types, rather than on a new trial. The FDA also issued a press announcement the same day, calling Anktiva the first FDA-approved treatment specifically for patients with BCG-unresponsive, high-grade papillary-only disease (FDA press announcement, October 14, 2026). ImmunityBio announced the approval on October 14, 2026 (8-K exhibit 99.1). The FDA says full prescribing information will be posted on Drugs@FDA; the letter had not been posted there when this page was published.
