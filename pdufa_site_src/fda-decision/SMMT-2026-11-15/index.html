The PDUFA goal date was November 14, 2026 (Summit 8-K, January 29, 2026). The FDA's Complete Response Letter is dated November 13, 2026, one day before the goal date; Summit announced it on November 15. Per Summit, the FDA found that substantial evidence of effectiveness is not established without a statistically significant overall survival improvement; HARMONi met its progression-free survival endpoint. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.
