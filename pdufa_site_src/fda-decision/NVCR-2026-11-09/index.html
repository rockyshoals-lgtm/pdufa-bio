Novocure announced on November 9, 2026 that the FDA issued a Not Approvable Letter on its Premarket Approval (PMA) application for Optune Mya in adults with brain metastases from non-small cell lung cancer following stereotactic radiation.

Daily close, T-120 (5/19/26) through T+2. Run-up high $19.99 on 7/23/26, low $13.11 on 10/29/26 (T-120 to T-1). Decision announced 11/9/26. Historical price action, not a forecast.

This is a medical-device PMA decision, not a drug application, so there is no PDUFA goal date and no Complete Response Letter. Per Novocure, the FDA cited uncertainty around the degree of clinical benefit and raised no safety concerns; Novocure plans to meet the FDA to discuss options that would not require additional clinical studies. The letter's own date is not public; the date on this page is Novocure's announcement.
