The PDUFA target action date was August 25, 2026 and the FDA approved the supplemental BLA that day. The approval covers all HER2-positive patients (IHC 3+ and IHC 2+/ISH+) regardless of PD-L1 status. It rests on the Phase 3 HERIZON-GEA-01 trial, published in the New England Journal of Medicine and first presented at ASCO GI 2026: both Ziihera-containing combinations improved progression-free survival in the overall HER2-positive population, reducing the risk of progression or death by 35%, with median PFS of 12.4 months against 8.1 months for trastuzumab plus chemotherapy. This is zanidatamab's second FDA approval in under two years. Zanidatamab is partnered: Jazz holds the US application, and Zymeworks (ZYME) is entitled to a $250 million milestone payment on this approval.
