Onswik (insulin efsitora alfa-gobe) was approved by the FDA on September 23, 2026 to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus, as an adjunct to diet and exercise, the 41st novel drug approval of 2026 on the FDA's Novel Drug Approvals list.

The FDA approved Onswik (insulin efsitora alfa-gobe), Lilly's once-weekly basal insulin for adults with type 2 diabetes, on September 23, 2026 (BLA 761408). Lilly announced the approval on September 24, 2026. It is on the FDA's 2026 Novel Drug Approvals list.

The FDA's approval letter for BLA 761408 is dated September 23, 2026 and states that Onswik is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (FDA approval letter); the letter gives the application as received September 23, 2025. Lilly announced the approval on September 24, 2026 (Lilly release). Per Lilly, the approval rests on the Phase 3 QWINT program, four trials in more than 3,400 adults with type 2 diabetes, in each of which once-weekly Onswik met its primary endpoint of non-inferior A1C reduction versus daily insulin glargine U-100 or insulin degludec U-100. Lilly says Onswik should not be used in type 1 diabetes and that the KwikPen will be available in the U.S. in the coming months in 500 and 1,000 units/mL strengths. pdufa.bio did not hold a goal date for this application, so no early or late margin is computed.
