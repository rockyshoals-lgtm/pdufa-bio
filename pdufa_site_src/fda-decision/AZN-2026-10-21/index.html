Ultomiris (ravulizumab) FDA approval for IgA nephropathy: Oct 21, 2026

Page updated November 5, 2026·next FDA decision on the calendar ·Data as of Nov 10, 2026 12:55 AM ET

Ultomiris (ravulizumab) - IgA nephropathy was approved by the FDA on October 21, 2026 for IgA nephropathy (reduce proteinuria).

Verified against: FDA action day October 21, 2026 (BLA 761108 S-046) · Announcement October 21, 2026 (AstraZeneca 6-K) · Goal date none sourced.

Ultomiris was approved in the U.S. to reduce proteinuria in adults with primary IgA nephropathy at risk of disease progression, under accelerated approval based on proteinuria reduction in the ongoing Phase III I CAN trial.

Drugs@FDA records BLA 761108 supplement 46 (efficacy) for Ultomiris as approved on October 21, 2026 (Drugs@FDA). AstraZeneca announced it in a 6-K on October 22, 2026 (AstraZeneca 6-K): the indication is approved under accelerated approval based on reduction of proteinuria, and it has not been established whether Ultomiris slows kidney function decline. Continued approval may be contingent on the ongoing I CAN Phase III trial, whose primary endpoint is change in eGFR at week 106; the pre-specified interim analysis showed a 43.4% reduction in proteinuria versus placebo at 34 weeks. AstraZeneca stated no goal date for this application, so no early or late margin is published; this calendar had carried it as a December 2026 row with no source.
