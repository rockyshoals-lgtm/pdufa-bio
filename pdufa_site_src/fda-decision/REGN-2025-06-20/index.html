Dupixent (dupilumab) FDA approval for bullous pemphigoid: Jun 18, 2025

✓ APPROVED

The FDA approved Dupixent (dupilumab) for adults with bullous pemphigoid on June 18, 2025, its eighth approved use in the US. Regeneron and Sanofi announced it on June 20.
