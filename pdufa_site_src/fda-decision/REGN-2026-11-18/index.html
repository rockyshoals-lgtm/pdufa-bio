Regeneron announced on November 18, 2026 that the FDA approved Livsayis (cemdisiran), given under the skin four times a year, for adults with generalized myasthenia gravis who are anti-acetylcholine receptor antibody-positive (Regeneron's announcement). The FDA's own record of the action day had not been posted when this page was published. Regeneron stated the target action date as November 2026 after using a Priority Review Voucher (Regeneron 8-K, July 30, 2026); because that is a month, not a day, no early or late margin is given. Per Regeneron, in the Phase 3 NIMBLE trial the MG-ADL score improved 4.5 points with Livsayis versus 2.2 with placebo at week 24 (p=0.0005), and 76.6% versus 44.1% of patients improved by at least 3 points. Livsayis carries a boxed warning for serious meningococcal infections and is available only through a REMS.
