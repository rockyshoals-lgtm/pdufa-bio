Telix announced on August 28, 2025 (Melbourne) that the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter on the BLA for Zircaix (TLX250-CDx), its PET imaging agent for characterizing renal masses as clear cell renal cell carcinoma.

Per Telix's release, dated Melbourne and Indianapolis, 28 August 2025, the CRL identifies deficiencies relating to the Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC) package, and notices of deficiency (Form 483) issued to two third-party manufacturing and supply chain partners will require remediation before resubmission (Telix release, SEC Exhibit 99.1). Telix set its investor call for 10:30 AM AEST on August 28, which was 8:30 PM Eastern on Wednesday August 27; the first U.S. trading day after the release was August 28. The letter the FDA has released for BLA 761401 is a corrected Complete Response Letter dated April 10, 2026; it states that it replaces the original CRL issued on August 27, 2025 and that the original action date stays the same (the letter's text). Telix's release states no PDUFA goal date, so no early or late margin is stated. A Complete Response Letter means the FDA will not approve the application in its present form; the sponsor can resubmit.

Questions

Was Zircaix (TLX250-CDx, 89Zr-DFO-girentuximab) approved by the FDA?

Not in this review cycle. On August 27, 2025 the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter, declining to approve the application for Zircaix (TLX250-CDx, 89Zr-DFO-girentuximab) in its current form. The company announced it on August 28, 2025. A CRL is not final; the primary source is linked on this page.

Which company is behind Zircaix (TLX250-CDx, 89Zr-DFO-girentuximab)?

Zircaix (TLX250-CDx, 89Zr-DFO-girentuximab) is a Telix Pharmaceuticals Limited (TLX) program. The FDA decision came on August 27, 2025; the FDA's own record of the decision is linked on this page.
