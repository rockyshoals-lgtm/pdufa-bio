All figures are medians of the events in that year. Run-up T-120→T-1 is the return from 120 trading days before the decision to the last session before it. Peak run-up is the return from that same T-120 close to the highest close anywhere in the window, which is what was actually on the table for anyone who sold into the run-up rather than holding to the last session. Run-up 30d is the same measured over 30 trading days. Decision day is the first session on/after the decision versus the prior close. T+5 is five sessions after the decision versus that same prior close. *2026 is a partial year.

How each year was checked. From 2025 every event is a decision in our archive with its own page and primary source (355 events). The 2020 to 2024 events (779) were re-checked on 2026-11-20 against the FDA’s records, the company’s own release or filing, or a dated report of the action, and rows that were not a PDUFA approval or CRL were removed (see Method & limits); they have no per-decision pages.

Method & limits

Universe: FDA decisions with usable daily price history, 2020-01-08 to 2026-11-24, one row per price event (two decisions for one company on the same day count once, and a move that answers an approval and a CRL together is left out because it belongs to neither). From 2025 every event is a decision in our published archive with a primary FDA, SEC or company source, and its outcome is the one that source states; records whose outcome was only inferred from the share price, whose cited source was re-read and did not state the FDA action, or whose own page says the label was wrong, are left out. The 2020 to 2024 events come from the research set the study began with. On 2026-11-20 each was re-checked against the FDA's own records (Drugs@FDA approvals, the FDA's published CRL letters), the company's own release or SEC filing, or for a few a dated trade report; each is dated to the day the action became public. Rows that were not a PDUFA approval or CRL (trial readouts, advisory votes, emergency use authorizations, tentative approvals, generic-drug and device actions), matched neither the action nor its announcement, counted one action twice, or were priced under a company with no stake in the drug were removed. These events have no per-decision pages on this site. Prices are split-adjusted daily closes (Polygon through 2026-11-09, FMP since; checked close by close at the switch). Returns are computed in trading days, not calendar days.

T-120 coverage. 1,100 of 1,134 events (97.0%) have a full 120 sessions of prior trading history; the rest are companies that had not been listed long enough, and they are excluded from the T-120 columns rather than measured over a short window. Coverage is 100% for 2026.

Limits worth stating. Daily closes understate intraday ranges, so every move here is a floor, not a ceiling. Medians hide dispersion, a typical move of a few percent coexists with a long tail of very large ones. A partial current year is marked with an asterisk and will move as more decisions land. Market-cap tier is assigned from the company's size at the time we recorded the event. This is a description of what happened historically; it is not a prediction and not investment advice.
