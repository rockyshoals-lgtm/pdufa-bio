All figures are medians of the events in that year. Run-up T-120→T-1 is the return from 120 trading days before the decision to the last session before it. Peak run-up is the return from that same T-120 close to the highest close anywhere in the window, which is what was actually on the table for anyone who sold into the run-up rather than holding to the last session. Run-up 30d is the same measured over 30 trading days. Decision day is the first session on/after the decision versus the prior close. T+5 is five sessions after the decision versus that same prior close. *2026 is a partial year.

Method & limits

Universe: every FDA PDUFA decision we have published in our decision archive with usable daily price history, 2020-01-08 to 2026-08-06. Prices are split-adjusted daily closes (Polygon). Returns are computed in trading days, not calendar days. Outcomes are taken from our published decision archive, each of which is sourced to a primary FDA, SEC or company filing.

T-120 coverage. 1,761 of 1,832 events (96.1%) have a full 120 sessions of prior trading history; the rest are companies that had not been listed long enough, and they are excluded from the T-120 columns rather than measured over a short window. Coverage is 100% for 2026.

Limits worth stating. Daily closes understate intraday ranges, so every move here is a floor, not a ceiling. Medians hide dispersion, a typical move of a few percent coexists with a long tail of very large ones. A partial current year is marked with an asterisk and will move as more decisions land. Market-cap tier is assigned from the company's size at the time we recorded the event. This is a description of what happened historically; it is not a prediction and not investment advice.
