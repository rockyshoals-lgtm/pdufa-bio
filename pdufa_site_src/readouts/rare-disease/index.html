This is a floor, not a complete census. 57 of the 351 upcoming readouts we track (16%) carry no therapeutic area, or are tagged only “Other”, and are therefore not counted on this page. Some of them are almost certainly rare disease. We would rather under-count against a field we can check than pad the number by guessing a therapeutic area from a drug name, which is how this site once published a discontinued 2017 compound as an upcoming event.

Questions

How many rare disease readouts are expected?
This page lists 20 upcoming rare disease clinical-trial readouts, covering November 2026 to May 2031. 14 of the dates are company-guided and 6 are estimated primary-completion windows from ClinicalTrials.gov. That number is a floor: 57 of the 351 upcoming readouts we track carry no therapeutic area or are tagged only “Other”, and are not counted here.
Are these rare disease readout dates exact?
Mostly no, and the page says which are which. A company-guided date is what the sponsor stated. An estimated date is the trial's primary-completion window from ClinicalTrials.gov, which is an estimate that moves. Where a source gave only a month or a quarter, this page shows a month or a quarter rather than inventing a day.
Does this page predict which readouts will be positive?
No. pdufa.bio publishes dates, sources and historical statistics. It does not publish approval odds or probability of success, and nothing here is investment advice.
