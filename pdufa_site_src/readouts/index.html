How many clinical trial readouts are tracked?

220 expected readouts are on the calendar as of October 4, 2026, each with its company-guided window and source.

Does pdufa.bio predict readout outcomes?

No. We publish dates, trial designs and measured stock reactions, each linked to its source; we do not publish outcome predictions or approval probabilities.

What is a readout?

The date a clinical trial's results become public, usually announced by the sponsor; unlike a PDUFA date it is an estimate until the company fixes it.
