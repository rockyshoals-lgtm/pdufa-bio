# July–August 2026 phase readouts — what is *actually* dated
**2026-07-12** · verified against company guidance, not just CT.gov

*Historical/observational data and company statements only. Not investment advice.*

---

## The thing you have to know first

**A phase readout has no scheduled date.** Unlike a PDUFA — which carries a legally-fixed FDA goal date — a company announces topline **when it decides to**. So "exact dates for all readouts" is a question with no honest answer for most of them.

Worse: **the 148 "exact days" in the mined file are the wrong event.** They are CT.gov **primary completion dates** — the day the last patient is measured for the primary outcome. That is a **data lock**, not an announcement. Topline lands weeks to months later. Publish the PCD as a readout date and you will be wrong, systematically, in the same direction.

Only three things produce a genuinely exact date. I chased all three.

---

## 1. Company guidance beats CT.gov — and it contradicted it on the top names

This is why the dig was worth doing. **Three of the biggest names in the file are wrong or misleading if you trust CT.gov alone.**

### 🔴 KOD — the August row is NOT the readout you think it is
CT.gov shows a Phase 3 tarcocimab row with an August 2026 lock. But **GLOW2 (diabetic retinopathy) already read out — 26 March 2026 — and it was POSITIVE**: 62.5% vs 3.3% achieving ≥2-step DRSS improvement; 85% risk reduction in sight-threatening complications. The August row is the *separate* wet-AMD program. **Do not file KOD as a pending GLOW2 readout.**

### 🟢 EYPT — the strongest genuine near-term readout on the board
DURAVYU **LUGANO + LUCIA** Phase 3 wet AMD. **Enrollment complete (>900 patients). Three consecutive positive DSMC recommendations.** Company guidance: **topline "mid-2026", LUGANO first, LUCIA shortly after.** Mid-2026 is *now*. Q2 earnings call **5 Aug**.

### 🟡 MIRM — the August lock is not a topline
The AZURE-1 Phase 2b **already reported**. Management: *"we'll have a lot more information with the full AZURE-1 and AZURE-4 Phase III data sets"* and **BLA submission in 2027**. The 1 Aug CT.gov date is not an announcement.
**But the transcript surfaced a real hard date:** MIRM has a **PDUFA of 26 September 2026** for **zilurgisertib** (FOP, licensed from Incyte, priority review). *Already on our calendar — verified.*

> **The lesson:** CT.gov tells you when a trial finishes. Only the company tells you when it will speak. When they disagree, the company wins.

## 4. Company-stated exact dates
**Essentially none exist.** Companies say "2H 2026," not "August 14." The one hard date this dig produced was a **PDUFA** (MIRM, 26 Sep) — a regulatory deadline, not a readout.
