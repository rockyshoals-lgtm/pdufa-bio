It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FDA. By continuing you agree to verify all dates and outcomes against primary sources — dates can slip and historical labels are still being validated.

HISTORIC DECISIONS — EXPERIMENTAL

Historic decisions are for research and education. Outcome and CRL labels are still being validated. Always cross-check company filings before making trading decisions.

What you're looking at. Every upcoming FDA decision (PDUFA) with the facts to weigh it — price & today's move, market cap, cash runway, the drug in plain English, and the current options market (expected move, IV, OI skew) vs the cohort base rate. Sorted by time-to-decision; tap any card for full detail & sources. Methodology: options via ORATS (ATM straddle → expected move); price via FMP; "Hist. move/LOA" are cohort base rates (694-PDUFA study, 2024–26), describing history — not this drug. Cash runway: red <6mo, amber 6–12mo (small/mid). Vol rich vs cohort / Vol low vs cohort = implied move ÷ the cohort-median move for that cap tier — context about premium richness vs history, not a recommendation. After a binary event, implied volatility collapses (IV crush), which can lose money on both long-vol and short-vol positions regardless of direction.
