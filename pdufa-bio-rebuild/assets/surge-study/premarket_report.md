# Pre-market edge test

_Informational / educational only - not investment advice._

Sample: 1880 gap-up events; 1851 (98.0%) had usable pre-market prints.

## Is the move already spent by 9:30?
- Median **pre-market share of the whole move** (prev close -> day high): **100.0%** (how much of the run is already done before the open).

## Does getting in pre-market beat buying the open?
| Entry -> exit | mean % | median % | win rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~09:00 pre-mkt -> 09:30 open | +5.7 | +1.3 | 58.9% |
| ~09:00 pre-mkt -> morning high (ideal) | +25.4 | +12.1 | 90.2% |
| 09:30 open -> morning high (ideal) | +18.4 | +9.5 | 95.0% |

**Read:** if 'pre-mkt -> open' is solidly positive and pre-market share of move is high, the run is mostly a pre-market event and the open-buyer is late. But the morning-high columns are *unattainable* upper bounds (you can't sell the exact high).

**Hard caveat:** micro-cap PRE-MARKET is extremely thin - quotes can be a few shares wide, many 'prints' aren't executable size, and you frequently can't get in or out at these prices. Treat pre-market numbers as the most optimistic/least-realizable in the whole study. Plus survivorship (currently-listed only).
