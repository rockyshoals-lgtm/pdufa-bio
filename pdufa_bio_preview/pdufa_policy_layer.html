Same standard as every other event on pdufa.bio: facts, sourced, dated, labeled.

How this layer works

01
Dated to the document
Every policy catalyst is pinned to the signing or effective date and linked to the primary source — the order, rule, or filing itself — not a headline about it.
02
Apolitical by design
We report the mechanism and the programs in scope. We don't tell you whether a policy is good, bad, or who it favors. No sentiment, no spin.
03
Effects are history, not forecasts
Any market reaction is shown as a dated fact that already happened — never a prediction of what a future policy will do to a price.

Executive Order FDA review pathway signed Apr 18, 2026
Accelerating Medical Treatments for Serious Mental Illness
Executive Order · The White House · directs FDA + DEA + HHS
PsychedelicsMental healthFDA priority reviewBreakthrough TherapyRight to Try

What the action does

  • Directs the FDA Commissioner to grant Commissioner's National Priority Vouchers to psychedelic drugs that already hold Breakthrough Therapy designation and meet the program criteria — compressing review once an NDA is filed.
  • Directs the FDA and DEA to establish a patient-access pathway under the Right to Try Act, including ibogaine compounds under FDA-authorized study.
  • Directs HHS to direct $50M toward federal–state collaboration on psychedelic research.
The order does not, by itself, approve any psychedelic drug or create enforceable patient/provider rights. It is a process-acceleration directive — which is exactly why we file it as a catalyst, not an outcome.
