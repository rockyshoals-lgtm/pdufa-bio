# The +5% Study — what actually predicts a 5% touch

**2026-08-28.** Two separate prediction problems, both validated walk-forward with nothing
fitted on the session it scores. Educational/informational, not investment advice.

---

## Headline

**BMO earnings: gap SIZE is the dominant predictor, and the relationship is V-shaped, not
linear.** Big gaps in *either* direction touch +5% about three times as often as flat gaps.
`|gap| ≥ 10%` → **57.2%**; `|gap| ≤ 3%` → **19.0%**. Difference **+38.1pp, z = 20.68,
p = 5.4×10⁻⁹⁵** on n = 5,470. This is the largest, best-sampled effect in anything we have
measured.

**Intraday board: `rvol_pace` is the filter that matters.** Base rate 12.4%; `rvol_pace ≥ 5`
→ **34.6%** (n = 254). Meanwhile `nscore`, the thing the board actually ranks on, has an
out-of-sample AUC of **0.557** — barely better than a coin flip at ranking.

---

## 1. BMO earnings — 5,470 reactions, 81 sessions (May 4 – Aug 27)

Every $300M+ earnings reaction with $3M+ traded, attributed to the session where the news
was live (BMO → same day, AMC → next day). Outcome: did the session high reach **+5% above
the open**. Baseline **29.0%**.

### The gap ladder

| gap band | n | touch +5% | closed green | median o2h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| < −15% | 174 | **58.0%** | 44.3% | +6.2% |
| −15 to −10 | 156 | **55.8%** | 48.7% | +5.7% |
| −10 to −5 | 442 | 42.8% | 52.9% | +4.3% |
| −5 to −3 | 358 | 29.6% | 50.0% | +2.9% |
| **−3 to 0** | 1397 | **18.6%** | 47.7% | +2.0% |
| **0 to +3** | 1790 | **19.4%** | 46.4% | +2.0% |
| +3 to +5 | 385 | 32.2% | 47.0% | +2.9% |
| +5 to +10 | 414 | 40.6% | 49.0% | +3.6% |
| +10 to +15 | 160 | **52.5%** | 53.1% | +5.4% |
| **> +15%** | 194 | **61.3%** | 47.9% | +6.5% |

A clean V. The floor is a *flat* gap, not a down gap.

### Why this does not contradict the board's ENTRY/EXTENDED rule — it answers a different question

The board says *"ENTRY = modest 2–6% gap, the sweet spot (57% closed green); EXTENDED =
gap >10%, the move is spent."* Look at the **closed green** column above: it is essentially
**flat at 44–53% across every band**. Gap size predicts **intraday RANGE**, not direction.

* Big gap → big intraday swing → frequent +5% touch, but the close is a coin flip.
* Flat gap → small range → rarely touches +5%, and the close is also a coin flip.

Both statements are true. The EXTENDED warning is answering the **hold-to-close** question.
**We scalp the touch.** For our strategy the EXTENDED penalty is pointed the wrong way.

### Best and worst cells

| cell | n | touch +5% | lift | median o2h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `|gap|≥10` AND EPS beat ≥25% | 248 | **61.3%** | +32.3pp | +6.5% |
| SMALL ($300M–2B) AND gap ≥ +10% | 131 | **61.1%** | +32.1pp | +6.4% |
| SMALL AND `|gap|` ≥ 10% | 252 | 59.5% | +30.5pp | +6.3% |
| SMALL AND gap ≤ −10% | 121 | 57.9% | +28.9pp | +5.7% |
| AMC reporter AND `|gap|` ≥ 10% | 166 | 56.6% | +27.7pp | +6.0% |
| MID AND `|gap|` ≥ 10% | 164 | 53.7% | +24.7pp | +5.6% |
| LARGE AND `|gap|` ≥ 10% | 101 | 40.6% | +11.6pp | +4.1% |
| SMALL AND `|gap|` ≤ 3% | 1004 | 23.6% | −5.4pp | +2.4% |
| **LARGE AND `|gap|` ≤ 3%** | 878 | **10.0%** | **−19.0pp** | +1.4% |

Size still matters on its own: SMALL 34.0%, MID 26.8%, **LARGE 15.0%**. And AMC reporters
beat BMO reporters (36.4% vs 31.1%), consistent with the earlier finding that the overnight
gap has already digested the news so more of the move is left after the open.

### Model

Walk-forward by calendar week, logistic regression on eps/rev surprise, gap, |gap|, gap
direction, log mcap, log dollar-volume, day-of-week, BMO flag.

```
OOS n=3543   AUC 0.7122   base 28.3%
  top 10% by model: 208/354 = 58.8%   lift +30.4pp
  top 20% by model: 378/708 = 53.4%   lift +25.1pp
```

Coefficients rank `logmc` (−0.493, smaller is better) and `absgap` (+0.409) far above
everything else. **EPS surprise is nearly worthless on its own (−0.026)** — the market's
reaction to the print carries the information, not the print.

## 2. Intraday board — 1,650 first-renders, 14 sessions

Every first render per (day, ticker) between 09:30 and 11:30, $300M+, ETFs excluded, all
features computed strictly *before* the render minute. Outcome: touched **+5% above the
render price** afterwards. Baseline **12.4%**.

### Simple rules

| rule | n | hit rate | lift |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`rvol_pace` ≥ 5** | 254 | **34.6%** | **+22.2pp** |
| rendered before 10:00 AND `rvol_pace` ≥ 3 | 386 | 28.0% | +15.6pp |
| has a real catalyst tag | 97 | 26.8% | +14.4pp |
| `rvol_pace` ≥ 3 | 478 | 24.7% | +12.3pp |
| `rvol_pace` ≥ 3 AND holding VWAP | 456 | 24.6% | +12.1pp |
| pre-armed (earnings/readout) | 106 | 21.7% | +9.3pp |
| rendered before 10:00 | 806 | 17.5% | +5.1pp |
| SMALL cap only | 687 | 15.7% | +3.3pp |

### Out-of-sample AUC

| scorer | OOS AUC |
|---|---|
| `rvol_pace` alone | **0.7274** |
| model, lean 4 features | 0.7221 |
| model, core 8 | 0.7103 |
| model, all 18 | 0.6857 |
| churn alone | 0.5812 |
| **`nscore` (incumbent)** | **0.5574** |

### But the model does NOT beat nscore on the book

Top-5 per session, out-of-sample, split ghosts removed: **model 19/50 (38%) vs nscore 18/50
(36%)** — 3 model wins, 2 nscore wins, 5 ties. That is a tie at n=50.

`rvol_pace` has the best AUC and the *worst* top-2 book (30%). No contradiction: AUC scores
the whole ranking, the book only cares about the extreme tail, and rvol_pace's extreme values
are not where the winners sit. **So rvol_pace is a filter, not a ranking.**

`churn` posted the best top-2 (55%) but only 13 of 1,650 rows carry `churn ≥ 5` — it is
mostly null on the board rows, so that number rests on a tiny effective sample. Do not act
on it until churn is populated.

## 3. Data-quality catch

Three records claimed >60% intraday moves after render: **MSTU +935%, REAX +916%, REAX
+909%**. Split ghosts, not moves. They inflated the model's "mean peak" from ~+4% to +95%
and would have manufactured an entire fake edge. Issues #69 / #143 confirmed live —
a corporate-action guard belongs in every study that reads daily or minute bars.

---

## What to change, and what not to

**Ship:**
1. **`rvol_pace` as a live column and a filter** — it is currently `null` on every board row
   despite being the single best predictor we have. Populate it, display it, and treat
   `≥ 3` as the minimum bar for a name to be actionable.
2. **Re-point the BMO gap logic.** Add a scalp-oriented tag driven by `|gap|`, and relabel
   the existing ENTRY/EXTENDED wording so it is clear it answers the hold question.
   `|gap| ≥ 10%` on a SMALL/MID cap is the best BMO cell we have (≈60%).
3. **A hard `|o2h| > 60%` sanity guard** on every bar-reading study.

**Do not ship:**
4. **Do not replace nscore's ranking with the model.** It does not beat it out-of-sample at
   n=50. The honest read is that nscore ranks poorly (AUC 0.557) *and* the model is not yet
   proven better on the only metric that pays. Both can be true.
5. **Do not act on churn's 55% top-2** until churn is actually populated.

**Open question worth the next study:** nscore at AUC 0.557 is doing very little work. The
right fix is probably to rebuild it around `rvol_pace` + catalyst presence + cap size rather
than to bolt a model on top — but that is a rebuild, and it needs its own walk-forward test
before it touches the live board.

# PART 2 — the edge hunt, and what shipped

Second pass over the same two datasets, looking for anything not already used. Every rule
below is measured; anything with n<40 is reported but not believed.

## Intraday, ranked by lift (n=1,650 renders, base 12.4%)

| rule | n | hit | lift | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`rvol_pace`≥5 AND rendered ≤30 min** | 228 | **36.0%** | **+23.5pp** | 3.8e-31 |
| `rvol_pace`≥3 AND a catalyst tag | 66 | 31.8% | +19.4pp | 1.1e-06 |
| **gapped DOWN but up ≥2% from open** | 105 | **28.6%** | **+16.1pp** | 2.2e-07 |
| `rvol_pace`≥3 AND rising into the render | 428 | 25.5% | +13.0pp | 2.0e-21 |
| premarket $vol ≥ $5M | 388 | 22.9% | +10.5pp | 7.0e-13 |
| rendered 09:30–09:45 | 505 | 22.0% | +9.6pp | 5.5e-15 |
| slope > 0.15 %/min into the render | 804 | 19.5% | +7.1pp | 1.5e-17 |
| **vs50 < −10 (deep downtrend)** | 240 | **18.8%** | **+6.3pp** | 1.3e-03 |
| **ANTI — `rvol_pace` < 1.5** | **704** | **4.4%** | **−8.0pp** | 1.6e-17 |
| rendered after 10:30 | 411 | 7.1% | −5.4pp | 1.4e-04 |

**The most useful single fact here is the anti-rule.** 43% of everything the board shows sits
below 1.5× its normal volume pace and hits +5% **one time in twenty-three**. Nearly half the
board is noise, and it is cheaply identifiable.

**Three results that contradict board folklore, all in the same direction:**
* `off_hi ≥ -1` (a name sitting at its high when we see it) is **negative**, −1.9pp.
* `from_open ≥ 5` (already extended) is **positive**, +5.9pp; "early in its move" (2–4%) is
  slightly negative.
* `vs50 < −10` (below the 50-day, the AMKR guard rail) is **positive**, +6.3pp.

All three say the same thing: for a **+5% touch**, what you want is a name with big range
today, not a tidy one. The AMKR guard rail and the ENTRY/EXTENDED labels are hold-to-close
rules and remain correct for that question.

## Earnings, ranked by lift (n=5,470, base 29.0%)

| rule | n | hit | lift |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AMC AND SMALL AND `|gap|`≥10** | 65 | **66.2%** | **+37.2pp** |
| `|gap|` ≥ 15 (any cap) | 368 | 59.8% | +30.8pp |
| `|gap|` ≥ 10 AND SMALL | 252 | 59.5% | +30.5pp |

| **EPS beat AND gap DOWN (the "v112 trap")** | 546 | **38.8%** | **+9.9pp** |

Two notes. The effect **plateaus at |gap|≥15** — 15, 20 and 25 all land near 60%, so there is
no reason to hold out for a bigger gap. And the **"v112 trap" is not a trap for a scalper**:
beat-with-a-gap-down runs +9.9pp above base for the +5% touch. It is a trap for a hold.

## Shipped to the live board

**`nest_egg.py`**
1. **`rvol_pace` early-session fix.** The old guard returned `None` for the first 7.8 minutes
   (10.3% of all first-15-minute rows). That is exactly the window that matters most. Now
   floored at one minute of session and flagged `rvol_pace_early` when still noisy.
2. **`rvol_pace` shown on the main table** with a hot/cold colour at the 3× line.
3. **QUALITY tag** — 🔥 PRIME / WARM / ❄ COLD, from the rules above.
4. **SCALP tag** — A/B/C/FLAT/FLAT-DEAD, from `|gap|` and cap size.
5. **↩ REVERSAL tag** — gapped down, now up ≥2% from the open.
6. ENTRY/EXTENDED tooltips rewritten to say they answer the **hold** question, with the
   scalp number alongside. Neither label was removed.
7. New fields logged (`q`, `scalp`, `absgap`, `reversal`, `rvol_pace_early`) so the next
   study can grade these without recomputing from bars.

**`armed_watch.py` (new)** — polls all ~385 armed READOUT/PDUFA/CONFERENCE names' snapshots
every 30s, independent of Polygon discovery, and logs any crossing +2% from the true open.
This closes the hole that ate **IOVA +16.0%, ZURA +20.7%, VERA +8.8%, JANX +6.7%** — all
armed, all ran hard, none ever appeared in the runners log. Verified: all four are in the
armed set and the snapshot reaches 327 of 385.

**`watchdog.py`** — now protects `top10_tracker` and `armed_watch`. Both are portless and
neither was watched; top10_tracker has died mid-session unnoticed at least twice.

**`RUN_ALL.bat`** — `armed_watch` added to the boards block; Chrome launched by explicit path
(the default handler on this machine is Edge, so the boards had been opening in the wrong
browser every morning).

## Still not shipped, deliberately

* nscore's ranking is unchanged. The model did not beat it out-of-sample on the top-5 book.
* churn stays out of any rule until it is actually populated (13 of 1,650 rows).
* Every number here is descriptive of 14 sessions and 5,470 earnings reactions. None of it
  is a forecast, and none of it is investment advice.
