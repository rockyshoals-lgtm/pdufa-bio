- It needs a full session live before either of us claims it made money.

I told you yesterday it would probably pay for itself in one trade. It might. It just
did not today, and I would rather say that than let a good story stand.

## What I changed tonight

**The board is now fluid.** You asked for it smaller and for the row count to follow
what is actually running. It used to render a hard 15 rows regardless, which meant a
quiet tape padded the screen with dead names and a wild tape truncated real ones.

Now a row has to earn its slot. It shows if it is firing GO, snapping back, has a
confirmed stack, is already up 10%+, is surging on live relative volume, or has a
tape thick enough to read. Floor of 5 so you always have something in front of you,
ceiling of 14 so it fits one screen. The sort still runs across every single name
first, so nothing live ever gets pushed off to make room for something quiet.

**Row density tightened** — smaller gaps and padding, same font sizes on the numbers
you read at a glance. That is worth about four extra rows of vertical room without
making anything harder to see.

Both changes verified with py_compile and a `node --check` on the extracted script
block before I touched anything else.
