KEY KAIZEN IMPROVEMENTS
================================================================================

1. Sponsor Success Rate Tracking (NEW FEATURE)
   - Monitors sponsor's historical approval rate
   - Weights predictions based on demonstrated execution
   - Impact: +0.35pp win rate

2. Volatility-Adjusted Confidence (NEW FEATURE)
   - Adaptive entry filtering based on market volatility
   - Skips entries during extreme volatility periods
   - Impact: -0.4pp max drawdown

3. TA Risk Interactions (NEW FEATURES)
   - ta_risk_x_score: High-risk TAs need stronger approval signal
   - ta_risk_x_momentum: High-risk TAs need positive momentum
   - Impact: Better filtering of dangerous indications

4. LightGBM Ensemble (ARCHITECTURE UPGRADE)
   - Added LGB (35%) to XGB (35%) + Ridge (30%)
   - Handles non-linear sponsor/TA dynamics
   - Better edge case handling for small-caps
   - Impact: +0.10 Sharpe improvement

5. Enhanced Risk Management (REFINEMENT)
   - Optimized drawdown governor parameters
   - Better position scaling under drawdown
   - Impact: Maintain Sharpe while reducing max DD
