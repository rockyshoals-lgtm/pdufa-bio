## RED TEAM: GAPS & MISSING CATALYST TYPES

### 🔴 CRITICAL GAPS (High Impact, Must Add)

1. **Conferences (0 events)** — Conferences drive massive biotech moves. ASCO alone can move stocks 20-50% on data presentations. Must add all 13+ major conferences above as calendar events, plus tag which tickers are presenting at each.

2. **AdCom Meetings (0 events)** — Advisory committee meetings are the single highest-volatility catalyst type. An AdCom vote can move a stock 30-60% in either direction. FDA publishes these ~15 days before the meeting. Need a process to scrape/add these as they're announced.

3. **Post-March PDUFA coverage** — The codebase has 51 PDUFAs but many are from the Feb-Mar window. Need to add all 30+ confirmed Q2-Q4 2026 PDUFAs documented above.

### 🟡 IMPORTANT GAPS (Medium Impact)

4. **Patent Cliff / LOE (Loss of Exclusivity) Events** — Major drugs going generic can crater stocks. Examples: Humira biosimilar wave, Keytruda patent expiry discussions. Institutional investors track these.

5. **IPO Lockup Expirations** — 180-day lockup expiry for recent biotech IPOs causes selling pressure. Easy to calculate, high signal for small/mid caps.

6. **Clinical Holds / Partial Holds** — When FDA issues a clinical hold, it's an immediate negative catalyst. Track ongoing holds and resolution dates.

7. **Breakthrough Therapy / Priority Review Designations** — These are announced ad hoc and often cause 10-20% moves in small caps. Currently captured as attributes of existing events but not as standalone catalyst events.

### 🟢 NICE TO HAVE (Lower Priority)

8. **M&A / Partnership Announcements** — Hard to predict timing, but tracking rumored deals or announced-but-not-closed acquisitions adds value.

9. **Insider Buying/Selling Clusters** — Already partially covered via the `market-data.js` API endpoint (FMP data), but not surfaced as calendar events.

10. **Capital Raises / Shelf Registrations** — ATM offerings and secondary offerings are dilutive catalysts that can be anticipated from S-3 filings.

11. **CMS / Reimbursement Decisions** — For approved drugs, CMS coverage decisions (NCDs/LCDs) are major commercial catalysts.

12. **International Regulatory (EMA, PMDA)** — European and Japanese approval dates for drugs already tracking US PDUFA.

## RECOMMENDED CALENDAR ARCHITECTURE

### Catalyst Types for the New Calendar

```
PDUFA          — FDA drug approval decisions (highest priority)
ADCOM          — FDA advisory committee meetings  
READOUT        — Clinical trial data readouts (Phase 1/2/3)
EARNINGS       — Quarterly earnings announcements
CONFERENCE     — Major medical/biotech conferences
DESIGNATION    — BTD/Priority Review/Orphan granted
PATENT         — Patent cliff / LOE events
IPO_LOCKUP     — IPO lockup expiration dates
```

### Filter Dimensions

```
By Date:        Today, This Week, This Month, Custom Range
By Type:        PDUFA, AdCom, Readout, Earnings, Conference
By Ticker:      Search/filter by stock symbol
By TA:          Oncology, CNS, Rare Disease, Immunology, CV, etc.
By ODIN Tier:   TIER_1, TIER_2, TIER_3, TIER_4
By Phase:       Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, NDA/BLA
By Designation: BTD, Orphan, Priority Review, Fast Track, RMAT
By Cap Size:    Micro (<$300M), Small ($300M-2B), Mid ($2B-10B), Large (>$10B)
```
