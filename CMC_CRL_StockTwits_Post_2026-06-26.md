When the FDA's only problem is the factory, not the drug 🏭

I pulled every FDA Complete Response Letter (CRL) from 2020–2026 that was driven ONLY by manufacturing / facility (CMC) issues — where the drug's own efficacy and safety were NOT in question — and checked each one against the FDA's published letter text. The pattern is one of the most durable edges in catalyst trading, but it comes with a trap most people miss.

THE PATTERN
Of 11 verified CMC-only CRLs that have reached a final decision, all 11 were eventually approved. 7 came back on the very NEXT review cycle — median ~10 months (range 6–13). The other 4 cleared after 2–3 cycles.

The receipts (single CMC-only CRL → approved next cycle):
• $CKPT Unloxcyt (cosibelimab) — 3rd-party CMO inspection → approved ~12 mo later
• $ALPMY Vyloy (zolbetuximab) — 3rd-party plant → ~9 mo
• $LLY Omvoh (mirikizumab) & $LLY Ebglyss (lebrikizumab) — facility/483 → ~7 & ~11 mo
• $SPPI Rolvedon, $ALVO Selarsdi, $REGN Lynozyfic — same story, all back within ~6–13 months
Multi-cycle but still approved: $VRCA Ycanth, $FENC Pedmark, $ALVO Simlandi, $ABBV Vyalev.

WHY IT HOLDS UP (it's not just my sample)
This is how CRLs actually behave. FDA's own analysis (Sacks et al., JAMA 2014, 151 first-cycle CRLs) found drugs rejected on SAFETY came back to approval 61.5% of the time vs just 31.3% for EFFICACY. A manufacturing CRL means the molecule already cleared efficacy and safety — you're fixing a plant, not re-running a trial. Pure CMC/labeling is only ~15% of all CRLs, but it's the most fixable slice. And resubmissions are quick: a Class 1 resubmission gets a 2-month FDA review, Class 2 gets 6 months.

THE HONEST CAVEATS (this is the part that keeps you out of trouble)
• It's a curated, named sample — NOT a random base rate. Don't read "11/11" as "100% odds."
• Most 2025–26 CMC CRLs are still pending (right-censored). They're not wins yet.
• A "CMC" label can hide a deeper problem. $ATRA / Pierre Fabre tab-cel (Ebvallo) fixed its factory after a Jan-2025 CMC CRL… then got a SECOND CRL in Jan 2026 — this time on EFFICACY (single-arm study deemed inadequate). ~18 months unapproved, Atara cut ~85% of staff and flagged going-concern. And $VRCA Ycanth was genuinely CMC-only but took 3 cycles over ~3 years. "Next cycle" is the mode, not a promise.

THE TELL
Read the actual CRL language. If the only issues are facility / CMC / Form-483 / third-party manufacturer — and the company explicitly says efficacy and safety weren't cited — that's the recoverable kind. The second a confirmatory trial or an efficacy/safety question shows up, it's a different animal (see tab-cel, $NVO insulin icodec's T1D miss, $REGN odronextamab's trial-enrollment CRL).
