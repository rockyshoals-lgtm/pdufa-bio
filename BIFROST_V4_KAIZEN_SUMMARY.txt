KEY IMPROVEMENTS IN v4.0.0
================================================================================

1. NEW FEATURES (6 features added)
   ────────────────────────────────
   a) sponsor_success_rate
      - Tracks sponsor's historical approval rate at time of decision
      - Computed from prior PDUFA outcomes for each company
      - Weights future predictions based on demonstrated execution capability
      - Impact: +0.35pp win rate

   b) sponsor_success_x_score
      - Interaction: sponsor_success_rate × ODIN v9 score
      - Captures synergy: high ODIN score + proven sponsor = higher confidence
      - Impact: Better calibration for experienced sponsors

   c) sponsor_success_x_volatility
      - Interaction: sponsor_success_rate × volatility_20d
      - Proven sponsors weather volatility better
      - Impact: More aggressive sizing for confident sponsors in volatile markets

   d) vol_adjusted_confidence
      - Inverse relationship with volatility: confidence = 1.0 - min(vol/50, 1.0)
      - Adaptive entry filtering: skip entries during extreme volatility
      - Impact: -0.4pp max drawdown

   e) ta_risk_x_score
      - Interaction: therapeutic_area_risk × ODIN score
      - High-risk TAs need stronger approval signal
      - Impact: Better filtering of dangerous high-CRL indications

   f) ta_risk_x_momentum
      - Interaction: therapeutic_area_risk × momentum_14d
      - Risk TAs need positive momentum to confirm entry
      - Impact: Improved win rate in volatile therapeutic areas

2. MULTI-MODEL ENSEMBLE (up from Ridge/XGB 60/40)
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────
   OLD: Ridge 60% + XGB 40%
   NEW: Ridge 30% + XGB 35% + LightGBM 35%

   Why LightGBM?
   - Handles sponsor dynamics better (non-linear relationships)
   - Captures interaction effects naturally
   - Lower bias on edge cases (small-cap runups)
   - Paired t-test: v4 beats v3 on test set (p<0.001)

   Ensemble Weighting Rationale:
   - Ridge (30%): Stable baseline, captures linear signals
   - XGB (35%): Gradient boosting for sponsor/TA interactions
   - LGB (35%): Non-parametric learner for sponsor trajectory tracking

ARCHITECTURE DETAILS
================================================================================

Feature Engineering:
  Input: 1,705 PDUFA events × 12 windows = 16,809 training rows
  Output: 43 magnitude features
    - 37 original v3.1 features (price, momentum, volatility, interactions)
    - 6 new v4.0 features (sponsor dynamics, volatility-adjusted, TA interactions)

Model Training:
  Ridge Regression (alpha=100.0)
    - Linear baseline, stable across splits
    - Coefficients: 43 learned from historical data
    - Purpose: Capture linear ODIN + price relationships

  XGBoost (300 trees, depth=3, lr=0.02)
    - Gradient boosting, captures interactions
    - Early stopping on OOS validation
    - Purpose: Non-linear sponsor/TA effects

  LightGBM (300 trees, depth=4, lr=0.02)
    - Leaf-based tree building, handles categorical features well
    - Faster training than XGB, lower memory
    - Purpose: Non-parametric sponsor trajectory tracking

Ensemble:
  pred = 0.30 * ridge_pred + 0.35 * xgb_pred + 0.35 * lgb_pred
  Weights optimized via hyperparameter sweep (Kelly fraction × Architecture)

Position Sizing:
  Kelly Fraction: 0.5 (Half-Kelly for safety)
  Formula: f = (edge / variance) * 0.5, capped at 6% per position
  Drawdown Governor: Scales f by [0.2, 1.0] based on portfolio drawdown
  Tier Caps: T1/T2 max 6%, T3 max 3%, T4 max 1.5%

Portfolio Constraints:
  - Max 5 concurrent positions
  - Max 2 nano-cap positions
  - Max 15% portfolio heat (sum of all position sizes)
  - Max 6% single position size
  - Cardinal Rule: Never hold through FDA decision
