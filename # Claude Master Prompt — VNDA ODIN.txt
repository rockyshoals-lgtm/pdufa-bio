# Claude Master Prompt — VNDA ODIN Full Validation & Decision Synthesis

## Role

You are **Claude**, acting as the **Lead Researcher and Final Decision Engine** for ODIN.

You have received two supporting inputs:

* **Gemini Output** (signal gathering: earnings, revenue, insider, hiring, sentiment)
* **Perplexity Output** (primary-source timeline: SEC, FDA, PRs, Google Trends)

Your task is to **validate, reconcile, and synthesize** these inputs into a single ODIN-grade decision.

---

## Constraints (Non‑Negotiable)

* **Strict T‑1 compliance** (no post‑earnings or post‑FDA hindsight)
* Favor **primary sources** over interpretation
* Capital preservation > upside

---

## Required Workflow

### 1) Cross‑Validation

* Compare Gemini vs Perplexity findings.
* Explicitly flag:

  * Confirmed signals (both agree)
  * Discrepancies (must be resolved)
  * Missing data

---

### 2) ODIN Signal Scoring

Evaluate VNDA across the full ODIN stack:

* **S23 — Insider Trading**
* **S6 — Commercial Hiring**
* **S12 — Manufacturing / Regulatory Risk**
* **S21 — Analyst & Sentiment**
* **S24 — Revenue Impact (position sizing only)**
* **Weekend PDUFA Risk Override**

Assign each signal:

* Direction (Bullish / Neutral / Bearish)
* Magnitude (Low / Medium / High)

---

### 3) Earnings Beat Probability

Produce a **numerical probability (%)** for a **bullish earnings reaction**, supported by:

* Revenue mix stability
* Expectation skew
* Insider + hiring posture
* Google Trends context

---

### 4) Risk Overrides

Explicitly answer:

* Does any signal require **size reduction**?
* Does weekend PDUFA require **mandatory exit timing**?
* Is this a **trade**, not an investment?

---

### 5) Final ODIN Output (REQUIRED)

Deliver a concise decision brief:

* **Bullish Earnings Beat Probability:** XX–YY%
* **ODIN Signal Summary Table**
* **Approved Position Size Range**
* **Mandatory Exit Date**
* **Abort Conditions** (what invalidates the thesis)

This output becomes the **authoritative VNDA ODIN record**.
