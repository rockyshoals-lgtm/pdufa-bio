# EMBERTHREAD — Story Bible v0.1

*Working title: **EMBERTHREAD** (alts: The Ninth Ember · The Last Turning · Unquenched)*
*Genre: Idle/incremental RPG · Tone: mythic & poetic · Mythos: Norse skeleton, cosmic-horror flesh*

---

## 1. Logline

The world is a burning thread, and a patient god is drawing a shroud over it. You are the one ember it could not swallow — reborn at the bottom each time the world ends, keeping a little more of your fire. Climb the Nine Realms across a thousand endings to find the single hidden seam where the shroud was tied to creation, and cut it — before you become part of it.

---

## 2. Voice & tone (the north star)

Mythic and poetic: sagas, invocations, prophecy. Short weighty lines. Cold beauty. The horror is never gore — it's *scale and indifference*. Grímnir is never loud; the calmer it speaks, the worse it is.

**Prologue invocation (cold open):**
> In the first age, the world was sung.
> In the last, it is being *unsaid*.
> Nine realms hang upon a burning thread, and the thread burns from the crown down.
> They call the fire above them a god. It has wagered a name — **Grímnir, the Hooded End**.
> It does not hate you. Winter does not hate the leaf.
> But you are the ember it did not swallow. And you remember the shape of the fire.

**The Turning (shown on every prestige/reset):**
> The thread burns through. The realms fall like beads from a cut string — and you fall with them, down to the ash-shore: smaller, colder, but not gone.
> You keep the heat. You always keep a little of the heat.

**Grímnir, spoken (rotate these at resets, boss gates, milestones):**
> "Again, little coal? Burn, then. I have all the endings; you have only your stubbornness."
> "You climb as though the crown were a door. It is a mirror."
> "I have unmade kinder things than you, and called it mercy."
> "Every spark believes it is the dawn. I have swept a billion dawns into the dark. Come — be a billion and one."
> *(late game)* "You are looking for the seam. So was I, once. Look what finding it made of me."

## 3. The mythos

- **The First Song** sang reality into being — nine realms, strung like beads on the **Emberthread**, a fuse that has always burned. Creation is not eternal; it is *slow-burning*.
- Beneath everything lies the **Hush** — the void that predates the Song. Not empty. *Waiting.*
- At the crown of the thread sits the **Hooded End**, drawing a shroud down over the realms. When the hood closes, the thread burns through and everything falls into the Hush.
- But the shroud will not close cleanly, because of a flaw at its own crown: the **Seam** — the first stitch that tied the ending to creation. While the Seam holds, the End cannot complete. So Grímnir *turns* the world instead — ending and relighting it, over and over, wearing it down toward the day the Seam finally frays.
- **You** are an **Ember**: a spark the shroud cannot smother, an error in the ending. Alone of all things, you keep your heat across the turnings — and therefore you *remember*.

---

## 4. The villain — GRÍMNIR, the Hooded End

**What it is:** Not a person; a *shroud with a will*. Older than the Song. It exists **outside the thread**, in the Hush, which is the source of its near-omnipotence — it can snuff any realm like a candle, rewrite what has happened, and reset the world at will.

**Why "almost" — the single crack:** It cannot close while the **Seam** holds, and it cannot find the Seam, because Grímnir exists *outside time* and the Seam is stitched *into* time. It half-forgets the Seam's location every turning. Only a being that moves *through* the realms in sequence — a climber, an ember with memory — can trace the map back to it. That being is you. This is the one thing in creation it cannot do for itself, and the one thing it cannot fully stop you from doing.

**Its nature (the cosmic-horror core):** Indifference, not malice. It ends things the way seasons turn. This makes it more frightening than any tyrant — you cannot bargain, enrage, or wound its pride, because it has none. Its "gentleness" is the horror.

**The late reveal (recontextualization):** Grímnir is not *destroying* the world. It is a **lid**. The shroud smothers the realms to keep the Hush from pouring *up* through them. Every turning buys creation a little more time. Grímnir is a near-omnipotent **guardian-tyrant** — and cutting the Seam may free the world, or may unleash exactly what the hood was holding back. This is the moral engine of the endgame.

**The dark mirror:** The shroud is *woven from climbers who reached the crown before you* and were unmade at the Seam. Every Ember who came closest became another thread in the hood. The nearer you get, the more you risk becoming the next stitch — the thing you're fighting is made of heroes who almost won.

---

## 5. The hero — the Ember-born (player)

Player-named. Identity: **"the one spark the dark forgot to swallow."** No chosen-one prophecy — the opposite. You are an *accident*, a defect in a perfect ending, and that defect is the only hope creation has. You are not special because you were chosen; you are special because you were *missed*.

Across turnings you gather other Embers — **the Unquenched** — failed climbers who kept a flicker. They are your party/hero collection, each carrying a fragment of their own doomed climb (and their own memory of the Seam).

## 6. Reset-as-story (mechanic/story fusion)

This is the spine — every core system is a story beat:

| Mechanic | Story |
|---|---|
| Auto-battle up the stage ladder | Climbing a realm along the burning thread |
| Boss gate | The **Warden** of each realm bars the ascent |
| Prestige / Ascend | A **Turning** — the world ends, you fall to the ash-shore, keep your heat |
| Meta-currency (Embers) | The heat you keep across turnings; "remembered fire" |
| Permanent multipliers | You burn hotter each turning — the shroud can't cool what it can't smother |
| Hero collection | The **Unquenched** — other Embers who climb with you |
| Offline earnings (capped) | The thread burns while you rest; but stay away too long and the shroud tightens |
| 2nd-layer prestige (late) | **Runes of the Seam** — fragments of the hidden weakness, recovered slowly |
| Endgame goal | Assemble the map, reach the crown in a single climb, cut the Seam |

**The elegant part:** you *cannot* reach the crown in one climb early — Grímnir resets you first. You reach it only by accumulating enough heat across turnings that a single ascent finally makes it all the way up. **The grind is literally the plot.** The player isn't grinding *toward* the story; the grinding *is* the story of wearing down an unwinnable war.

## 7. The Nine Realms (the ascension ladder)

Norse cosmology reimagined as stages of the world's death — a climb from the dead-bottom to the god-crown. Each is a boss tier with a Warden.

| # | Realm | Reimagined as | Warden (boss) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Helstrand** — the Ash-Shore | Where forgotten embers wash up; you begin here each Turning | The Ferryman of Cinders |
| 2 | **Nifgrave** — the Cold Below | Frozen mist; the slow, quiet death (Niflheim) | The Rime Mother |
| 3 | **Myrkviðr** — the Black Wood | Petrified forest of the dark-elf ruin (Svartalfheim) | The Root-that-Gnaws |
| 4 | **Járnvarð** — the Iron Waste | Graveyard of the giants; dead titans as terrain (Jotunheim) | The Last Jötun |
| 5 | **Vanhal** — the Drowned Garden | Once-fertile Vanaheim, now sunken and rotting; nature turned wrong | The Bloom Unmade |
| 6 | **Ljósfell** — the Glass Reaches | Alfheim's light gone blinding and hollow; beauty as threat | The Choir of Glass |
| 7 | **Middengarð** — the Hollow World | The mortal world (Midgard), emptied of every soul; eerily *ours* | The Echo of Kings |
| 8 | **Gullthróne** — the Gilded Ruin | Asgard fallen; dead gods still seated on their thrones | The Throned Corpse |
| 9 | **The Crown** — Grímnheim, the Hooded Height | Where Grímnir sits and the Seam is hidden | Grímnir / the Seam itself |

Realms 1–8 are the repeatable climb; **the Crown** is only reachable once you're strong enough to ascend without falling — the whole game bends toward first setting foot there.

## 8. The hidden weakness — the Seam (the long hunt)

- Each realm hides a **Stitch-Rune** — a fragment of the map to the Seam, revealed only by climbing far enough into that realm.
- Collected across turnings, the runes assemble into the **Crown-Map**. This is the meta-goal that gives hundreds of hours of climbing a *destination*.
- The Seam can only be cut at the crown, in a single unbroken ascent — the ultimate expression of "a hidden weakness at the very top of the ladder."
- Recovering runes is gated by *both* progress and turnings, so the hunt naturally paces the whole lifespan of a player's account.

## 9. Twists & the three endings (replay + live-content hooks)

**Seeded twists:**
1. *You are not the first.* The Unquenched you recruit are prior climbers who failed — proof you could climb forever and lose.
2. *The hood is made of heroes.* Those who reached the Seam and were unmade became threads in the shroud. Getting close is dangerous to your soul, not just your life.
3. *Grímnir is a lid.* The final reveal: the End has been holding back the Hush. Ending it may doom the world it was smothering.

**Three endings (unlock at increasing turning-counts — natural long-tail goals):**
- **Cut the Seam** — the shroud fails, the world is free… and the Hush pours up through the realms. *Bittersweet / dark.*
- **Wear the Hood** — take Grímnir's place to hold the Hush back yourself. You become the next Hooded End; a new Ember will one day climb toward *you*. *Tragic mirror — the loop never ends, only changes hands.*
- **Reweave the Seam** *(true ending, hardest)* — neither free nor smother: mend the stitch, re-tie ending to creation on *new* terms, and let the thread burn without falling. Requires the most turnings and all runes. *Hard-won hope.*
