# 4. ⚠️ AI CITATIONS: growing deeper, not wider

Citations rose 2.2K → 2.4K, but **grounding queries stayed at 18.** Avg cited pages went 13 → 14.

**Read that carefully: we are being cited more often on the queries we already own, and no new queries entered.** The deepening is good; the plateau in breadth is the thing to fix.

**Breadth comes from entities.** 9 of 18 grounding queries are `{drug} pdufa date`. Every drug page with a clean answer sentence is a candidate query. The lever remains what it has been for two weeks: **`Drug` schema + `alternateName` across 544 pages**, which is still absent.

*Note: `fda approvals today pdufa` is flat at 36 citations. `/fda-decisions-today` only shipped Sept 1 — too early to read. Check it Sept 8.*

# 5. HOW TO KEEP CLIMBING

## 🥇 Fix the zero-click tier — free, no ranking risk
~130 impressions/month at positions 3.7–8.0 producing nothing. **Rewrite titles and meta descriptions on the four page types above.** Start with the decision-page template — it's entering the index now and scales to 455 pages.

The test for each: *does the snippet answer the query, or just name the page?* `/fda-decision/RARE-2026-08-19` should read like *"RARE's DTX401 was approved August 19, 2026 — four days early."* Not "FDA Decision: RARE."

## 🥈 Make `/learn/what-is-a-pdufa-date` win its own term
Third audit at zero. At position 8 it will never convert. It needs to reach top 3, and the route is internal links — **every one of the 544 drug pages and 96 event pages should link it on first use of "PDUFA."** That's the cheapest authority transfer available and it's entirely internal.

## 🥉 `Drug` schema + `alternateName` — the breadth lever
Grounding queries have plateaued at 18. Entity markup is how you add more. `FAQPage` says *"there's a Q&A here"*; `Drug` says *"this page **is** camizestrant."* Still absent on 544 pages after two weeks.

## 4. Ship the six stale decided pages
MRNA, TAK, BMY, JAZZ, ZYME, GILD still show pending decisions. `/pdufa/JAZZ` earns 37 impressions at position 2.70 — **a high-ranking page serving wrong information** is worse than one that ranks poorly.

## 5. Link the 458 CRL letters
Still zero `fda.gov` links. Decision pages are entering the index right now; adding the primary-source letter to that template as it scales is better timing than retrofitting later.

## 6. Google: do not chase the head terms
`pdufa dates` position **68.8**, `pdufa date` **81.3**. Meanwhile every entity query converts at 50–100%. **Indexed has been 57 for fifteen days** — content volume is not the lever; links are, and you're handling those.
