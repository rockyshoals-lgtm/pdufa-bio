The Counterintuitive Finding: CRLs Run Up More

Conventional wisdom says stocks run up more before approvals. The data says otherwise. Across 1,705 PDUFA events from 2020–2026, CRL-destined stocks average a +1.93% fourteen-day runup, more than double the +0.86% for approval-destined stocks.

Bull Trap Alert: Speculative buying pressure flows disproportionately into lower-probability names before PDUFA dates. The bigger the pre-event runup, the more likely it represents retail FOMO rather than informed positioning.

This pattern holds across all lookback windows from 3 to 30 days. The spread is widest at 30 days (CRL +3.99% vs. Approval +2.13%) and narrows at shorter intervals, but CRLs consistently outpace approvals in pre-event price appreciation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PDUFA runup?

A PDUFA runup is the stock price increase in the days and weeks before an FDA PDUFA date. Biotech stocks often see buying pressure ahead of binary FDA decisions as traders position for potential approval. The typical 14-day runup averages +1.17% across all events.

Do approval-destined stocks have bigger runups?

No. CRL-destined stocks average +1.93% (14-day) versus +0.86% for approvals. Speculative buying disproportionately targets lower-probability, higher-volatility names, creating bull traps.

Which ODIN tier has the best entry for PDUFA trades?

T1 events (score above 0.85) with below-average runups offer the strongest risk-adjusted entry: 93% approval rate, near-zero pre-event runup, and positive post-event drift (+0.30%).

What is a PDUFA bull trap?

A PDUFA bull trap occurs when a low-probability stock surges before its catalyst date, attracting speculative buyers, only to crash on a CRL. T4 events with 14-day runups above 10% average +25% runup but only 21% approval rate.

How does ODIN score PDUFA events?

ODIN v5 is a 25-feature logistic regression model trained on 2,200+ historical PDUFA events. It considers sponsor experience, regulatory designations (BTD, Priority Review, Orphan), prior CRLs, therapeutic area risk, and interaction effects to produce an approval probability score from 0 to 1. It achieves AUC 0.9007 on holdout validation.
