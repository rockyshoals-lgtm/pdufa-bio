FEATURES ENGINEERED (34 NEW FEATURES)
================================================================================

CATEGORY 1: ENDPOINT GRANULARITY (10 features)
   Binary: ep_is_os, ep_is_pfs, ep_is_orr, ep_is_safety, ep_is_biomarker,
           ep_is_pk_pd, ep_is_qol
   Count:  num_primary_outcomes, num_secondary_outcomes, num_total_outcomes
   
   RATIONALE: Endpoint type is critical for readout success
   - OS = gold standard, lowest uncertainty (positive signal)
   - ORR = binary response, variable signal (depends on indication)
   - Biomarker = mechanistic only, risky in phase 3 (negative signal)

CATEGORY 2: TRIAL TIMING (3 features)
   primary_timeframe_days (62.4% coverage, imputed from phase median)
   time_start_to_primary_completion (100% coverage)
   time_to_readout_days (100% coverage, calendar delay risk)
   
   RATIONALE: Timing captures durability, statistical delay, and execution risk
   - Long timeframe = robust data but calendar risk
   - Short timeframe = quick signal but higher variance
   - Calendar delays = often correlate with negative results

CATEGORY 3: TRIAL STRINGENCY (4 features)
   inclusion_criteria_count (100% coverage)
   exclusion_criteria_count (100% coverage, mostly 0)
   total_criteria_count (100% coverage)
   log_elig_text_length (100% coverage, 7,271 unique values)
   
   DERIVED: stringency_score = inclusion + 2×exclusion + 0.001×text_length
   
   RATIONALE: Restrictive eligibility limits population, affects recruitment
   - Complex criteria = smaller N, more homogeneous (positive for signal)
   - But also slower accrual, recruitment failures (negative risk)

CATEGORY 4: INTERVENTION TYPES (5 features)
   has_drug (100% coverage)
   has_biological (100% coverage)
   has_genetic (100% coverage)
   has_combination (100% coverage)
   has_active_comparator (100% coverage)
   
   RATIONALE: Intervention complexity affects regulatory expectations
   - Small molecule = straightforward, well-understood
   - Biologic = immunogenicity/manufacturing risk
   - Genetic = innovative but novel (risky)
   - Combination = higher toxicity risk
   - Active comparator = higher efficacy bar (harder to show superiority)

CATEGORY 5: COMPARATOR DESIGN (2 features)
   has_sham_comparator (100% coverage)
   comparator_richness (derived: 0, 1, or 2)
   
   RATIONALE: Comparator design affects statistical rigor
   - Sham/placebo = easier to show efficacy (but invasive)
   - Active = gold standard but higher efficacy bar

CATEGORY 6: SPONSOR & COLLABORATION (6 features)
   is_industry (100% coverage)
   is_nih (100% coverage)
   is_academic (100% coverage)
   has_industry_collab (100% coverage)
   is_fda_regulated_drug (100% coverage)
   num_collaborators (100% coverage, 0-21 co-sponsors)
   
   RATIONALE: Sponsor type affects regulatory standards and execution
   - Industry = higher FDA alignment, better resources (positive)
   - NIH = rigorous but variable
   - Academic = exploratory, often underpowered
   - Collaboration = risk-sharing, de-risks single-sponsor bias

CATEGORY 7: ENROLLMENT & RECRUITMENT (2 features)
   is_actual_enrollment (100% coverage, 0/1 binary)
   healthy_volunteers (100% coverage, 0/1 binary)
   
   RATIONALE: Recruitment signals trial execution and design
   - Actual enrollment = transparent, credible (positive)
   - Healthy volunteers = unusual for efficacy (signals safety focus)

INTERACTIONS (11 features, plus 1 derived)
   Phase × Endpoint:
   - phase3_x_os (strongest positive signal: pivotal with OS)
   - phase3_x_orr (positive: pivotal with ORR)
   - phase3_x_biomarker (negative: biomarker in pivotal = underdeveloped)
   - phase2_x_biomarker (positive: biomarker PoC in phase 2)
   
   Sponsor × Design:
   - industry_x_double_blind (strong positive: FDA-preferred)
   - industry_x_randomized (positive: reduces bias)
   - academic_x_single_arm (neutral: common for academic PoC)
   
   Design × Complexity:
   - stringency_x_large_trial (positive: refined + powered)
   - biomarker_x_enrollment (negative: biomarkers harder in large trials)
   
   Special:
   - onc_x_pk_pd (negative: no clinical endpoint in oncology)
